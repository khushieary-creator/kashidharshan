A Kashi Dharshan tour package starts from ₹7,900 per person for a 3-day / 2-night trip, including stays, private travel, a guide and darshan assistance. The final price depends on hotel category, group size and season — larger groups pay less per person.
How many days do you need for Kashi darshan?+
2 nights / 3 days is ideal for an unhurried visit to the Kashi Vishwanath Dham, Ganga Aarti, Assi Ghat, Annapurna Mandir and Manikarnika Ghat. Add 1–3 days to also cover Ayodhya, Prayagraj or Sarnath.
How far is Ayodhya from Varanasi (Kashi), and how many days are needed?+
Ayodhya is about 200–230 km from Varanasi — roughly 5–6 hours by road. A combined Kashi–Ayodhya tour package is comfortable in 4 days / 3 nights, from ₹12,900 per person.

Can you arrange VIP or fast-track Kashi Vishwanath darshan?+
Yes. Wherever the temple administration offers sugam / suvidha darshan, we arrange priority entry, aarti seating, and senior or wheelchair assistance so you spend your time in prayer, not in queues.

🛕 Complete Kashi Vishwanath & Varanasi Sacred Yatra Encyclopedia 2026

Welcome to Kashi Dharshan, the official local pilgrimage travel agency dedicated to arranging seamless, spiritual, and VIP-guided yatras across Varanasi (Kashi), Ayodhya, Prayagraj, Vindhyachal, Chitrakoot, Naimisharanya, Mathura, and Vrindavan. Known as Mokshada (the city of liberation) and the eternal abode of Lord Shiva, Kashi is the oldest living city in the world. Our mission is to ensure every yatri experiences the divine peace of Kashi Vishwanath, the mystical illumination of Ganga Aarti, and the serene heritage of Sarnath without queues, confusion, or transport stress.

1. Detailed Varanasi & Sarnath Pilgrimage Itineraries

🚩 Option 1: Same-Day Express Kashi Vishwanath & Ganga Aarti Tour (1 Day)

  • Morning (06:00 AM – 10:00 AM): Arrival at Varanasi Airport (VNS) or Varanasi Junction (BSB). Private AC cab transfer to hotel for freshening up. Proceed to Gate No. 4 (Chhatta Dwar) for fast-track Sugam Darshan VIP Pass entry to Kashi Vishwanath Temple. Visit Annapurna Devi Mandir & Kaal Bhairav Mandir (the Kotwal of Kashi).
  • Afternoon (11:30 AM – 03:30 PM): Drive to Sarnath (10 km). Explore Dhamek Stupa, Mulagandha Kuti Vihar, Chaukhandi Stupa, and the Archaeological Museum housing the Ashoka Lion Capital.
  • Evening (04:30 PM – 08:30 PM): Board a private hand-rowed or motor boat at Dashashwamedh Ghat for the world-famous evening Ganga Aarti & Bajra cruise. Experience 21 priests performing synchronized lamp rituals. Departure transfer to airport or railway station.

🚩 Option 2: Classic Kashi Heritage & Sarnath Pilgrimage Tour (3 Days / 2 Nights)

  • Day 1: Arrival, VIP Darshan & Evening Ganga Aarti: Pickup from VNS Airport / BSB Station. Hotel check-in. Afternoon Kashi Vishwanath Sugam Darshan & Kaal Bhairav temple visits. Evening private boat cruise for Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti. Overnight stay in Varanasi.
  • Day 2: Subah-e-Banaras, Ghat Boat Ride & Sarnath Heritage: Early morning 05:30 AM Subah-e-Banaras sunrise boat ride from Assi Ghat to Manikarnika Ghat. Breakfast at hotel. Visit Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, Tulsi Manas Mandir, Tridev Mandir, and BHU Vishwanath Temple (New Kashi Vishwanath). Post-lunch Sarnath Buddhist circuit tour. Shopping for authentic Banarasi Silk Sarees at Godowlia market. Overnight stay in Varanasi.
  • Day 3: Departure Transfer: Morning breakfast, free time for local street food tasting (Kachori-Jalebi at Chachi ki Dukan, Malaiyyo in winter), and airport/railway station departure.

1. Verified 1-Day, 2-Day, 3-Day & 5-Day Kashi & Sub-Circuit Yatra Itineraries

Option A: 1-Day Express Kashi Vishwanath & Ganga Aarti Itinerary

Ideal for travelers with limited time arriving early morning at Varanasi Junction (BSB) or Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS).

  • 05:00 AM – 07:00 AM: Arrival & Holy Dip: Arrive in Varanasi. Cab transfer to Godowlia / Dashashwamedh Ghat. Take a sacred morning dip in the Holy River Ganga (Ganga Snan) at Dashashwamedh Ghat or Rajghat.
  • 07:30 AM – 10:30 AM: Kashi Vishwanath VIP Darshan & Annapurna Temple: Enter Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor via Gate No. 2 (Saraswati Gate) or Gate No. 3 (Bansphatak Gate). Enjoy Sugam Darshan VIP entry to offer Jalabhishekam at the Golden Jyotirlinga. Visit Maa Annapurna Mandir next door for divine blessings and free Mahaprasadam.
  • 11:00 AM – 01:00 PM: Kal Bhairav Mandir (Kotwal of Kashi): Head to Kal Bhairav Temple to seek permission from the guardian deity of Kashi. Tie the sacred Black Thread (Ganda) for protection.
  • 01:30 PM – 03:00 PM: Authentic Banarasi Lunch & Vishwanath Gali Shopping: Relish traditional Banarasi Thali, Kachori Sabzi, Malaiyyo (seasonal sweet), and Rabri Lassi near Chowk. Browse Vishwanath Gali for Banarasi Silk Sarees, Rudraksha malas, and brassware idols.
  • 03:30 PM – 05:30 PM: Sankat Mochan, BHU Vishwanath & Durga Mandir: Private AC cab excursion to Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple (offering Besan Ladoo), Durga Kund Temple, and New Vishwanath Temple (VT) inside Banaras Hindu University (BHU) campus.
  • 06:00 PM – 07:30 PM: Grand Private Ganga Aarti Boat Ride: Board a private wooden boat or Bajra at Assi Ghat or Dashashwamedh Ghat. View the magnificent 7-pandit grand evening Ganga Aarti from the riverfront amidst thousands of floating diyas.
  • 08:30 PM: Departure: Night transfer to Airport or Railway station with sacred memories and Prasad.

Option B: 2-Day / 1-Night Complete Kashi Heritage & Sarnath Pilgrimage

The most popular short weekend package for working professionals and families.

  • Day 1 – Kashi Temple Circuit & Ganga Aarti: Morning hotel check-in near Kashi Vishwanath Corridor. Attend afternoon VIP Kashi Vishwanath Darshan, Annapurna Temple, and Kal Bhairav. At 05:30 PM, board a private motorboat from Dashashwamedh Ghat to view Manikarnika Ghat (the eternal pyre), Harishchandra Ghat, and the illuminated evening Ganga Aarti. Night stay at Varanasi hotel.
  • Day 2 – Subah-e-Banaras, Sarnath Excursion & Departure: Wake up at 05:00 AM for Subah-e-Banaras sunrise boat tour at Assi Ghat with Vedic chanting and classical music. At 10:00 AM, proceed on a 10 km trip to Sarnath where Lord Buddha preached his first sermon after enlightenment. Explore Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, Ashoka Pillar original lion capital at Sarnath Archaeological Museum, and Thai Temple. Evening transfer to Airport / Station.

Option C: 3-Day / 2-Night Grand Kashi & Dev Deepawali Pilgrimage Yatra

The ultimate immersive Kashi experience including Mangla Aarti, VIP Corridor tour, Ganga Cruise, and Ramnagar Fort.

  • Day 1 – Arrival, Ghat Walk & Evening Ganga Aarti Boat Ride: Pickup from VNS Airport / BSB Station. Hotel check-in. Evening heritage walking tour across 84 Ghats from Assi to Dashashwamedh. Enjoy 2-hour private boat ride for evening Aarti and Manikarnika Mahasmashan darshan. Overnight stay.
  • Day 2 – 03:00 AM Mangla Aarti & Sarnath Sightseeing: Early morning 03:00 AM entry for divine Kashi Vishwanath Mangla Aarti (prior booking required). Return for breakfast. Afternoon trip to Sarnath Buddha Circuit & Archaeological Museum. Evening free for shopping Banarasi Silk Sarees and Zari handicrafts. Overnight stay.
  • Day 3 – Ramnagar Fort, Markandey Mahadev & Departure: Morning visit to Ramnagar Fort across the Ganga River, famous for Vintage Car collection and Ramlila museum. Optional trip to Markandey Mahadev Temple (where Ganges meets Gomti river, 28 km from city). Afternoon return and airport/railway drop.

Option D: 5-Day / 4-Night Sacred Uttar Pradesh Triangle (Kashi – Prayagraj – Ayodhya)

A complete spiritual circuit connecting Lord Shiva’s Kashi, Triveni Sangam Prayagraj, and Lord Ram’s Ayodhya Dham.

  • Days 1 & 2 – Varanasi (Kashi Dham): Full 2 days dedicated to Kashi Vishwanath VIP Darshan, Kal Bhairav, Sarnath, and Ganga Aarti Boat ride.
  • Day 3 – Prayagraj Triveni Sangam Day Excursion (125 km): Drive to Prayagraj. Take a private wooden boat for Sangam Holy Dip at the confluence of Ganga, Yamuna & mythical Saraswati. Visit Lete Hanuman Temple, Anand Bhawan (Nehru ancestral home), and Alopi Devi Shaktipeeth. Return to Kashi or proceed to Ayodhya.
  • Days 4 & 5 – Ayodhya Ram Mandir Dham (200 km): Proceed to Ayodhya. Seek blessings at Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir (VIP Pass entry), Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and attend evening Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi. Night stay in Ayodhya and return transfer on Day 5.

2. Complete Kashi Vishwanath Sugam Darshan & Mangla Aarti VIP Pass Booking Guide

Visiting Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga without standing in long queues (which can stretch up to 3-5 hours on Mondays and festival days) requires understanding the official VIP ticket system managed by the Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust (SKVT).

Official Pass Categories & Ticket Prices:

Darshan / Aarti Category Official Fee (Per Person) Timing / Slots Key Highlights & Rules
Sugam Darshan (VIP Entry Pass) ₹300 (Normal Days) / ₹500 (Sawan Mondays) 06:00 AM to 06:00 PM (Hourly Slots) Dedicated fast-track VIP queue bypasses general crowd. Takes 15-25 minutes total. Includes Angavastram/Prasad.
Mangla Aarti Pass ₹500 - ₹1,000 (Sawan Surge: ₹1,500 - ₹2,000) 03:00 AM – 04:00 AM (Entry 02:30 AM) First divine awakening ceremony of Lord Shiva. Sit inside Garbhagriha. Extremely limited seats (Must book 30 days prior).
Bhog / Midday Aarti ₹300 11:15 AM – 12:20 PM Sacred food offering ceremony. High spiritual energy inside inner sanctum.
Saptarishi Aarti Pass ₹300 07:00 PM – 08:15 PM Performed simultaneously by 7 chief archakas representing the Seven Sages. Deeply mesmerizing Vedic chants.
Shringara Aarti Pass ₹300 09:00 PM – 10:15 PM Night decoration ceremony where Jyotirlinga is adorned with grand floral crowns and sandalwood paste.

Step-by-Step Online Booking & Entry Gate Rules:

  1. Official Booking Website: Visit the official portal shrikashivishwanath.org or let Kashi Dharshan manage ticket issuance directly with your package booking.
  2. ID Verification: Valid original Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID is mandatory at entry gates matching the ticket name.
  3. Entry Gates: Gate No. 1 (Dundhiraj/Ganesh Gate via Chowk), Gate No. 2 (Saraswati Gate via Vishwanath Gali), Gate No. 3 (Bansphatak Gate), and Gate No. 4 (Ganga Riverfront Corridor Gate for boat arrivals).
  4. Strict Locker & Prohibited Items Policy: Mobile phones, electronic gadgets, smartwatches, metal objects, leather belts, shoes, and big bags are strictly prohibited inside the inner sanctum. Free locker counters are provided by the Trust at Gate 2 and Ganga Riverfront.
  5. Dress Code for Sparsh Darshan (Abhishek): To perform direct touch Sparsh Darshan or Jalabhishekam inside the Garbhagriha, male devotees must wear traditional Dhoti-Kurta (unstitched lower garment) and female devotees must wear Saree or Salwar Suit. Leather shoes or Western casual attire like shorts are forbidden inside the temple core.

7. Sacred Geography of Kashi Dham & Exhaustive Guide to 84 Riverfront Ghats

Kashi (Varanasi) is recognised as the oldest living city in human civilization, existing for over 5,000 years continuously on the crescent-shaped western bank of the sacred River Ganga. In Skanda Purana's Kashi Khanda, Shiva declares Kashi as His permanent abode — Anandavana (the Forest of Bliss) and Avimuktha Kshetra (the sacred territory that Shiva never forsakes, even during Pralaya or global cosmic dissolution).

The Pancha Tirthas (5 Most Sacred Riverfront Ghats of Kashi):

Performing holy dips and offering Pinda Daan ancestral rituals across these 5 key ghats completes the sacred Pancha Tirtha Yatra:

  • 1. Assi Ghat (Confluence of River Assi & Ganga): The southernmost major ghat where Goddess Durga dropped her sword (Asi) after destroying demons Shumbha and Nishumbha. Famous for Subah-e-Banaras sunrise morning Vedic chants, Yogasana sessions, and classical music performances.
  • 2. Dashashwamedh Ghat (The Heart of Kashi): Where Lord Brahma performed ten Horse Sacrifices (Dasha Ashwamedha Yajna) to welcome Lord Shiva to Kashi. The venue for the famous grand 7-priest evening Ganga Aarti.
  • 3. Manikarnika Ghat (The Holy Burning Ghat & Shaktipeeth): The primary cremation ghat where Lord Shiva grants Taraka Mantra for instant Moksha (liberation from the cycle of rebirth). According to Puranas, Goddess Sati's earring (Manikarnika) fell into the sacred Chakra Pushkarini Kund excavated by Lord Vishnu.
  • 4. Panchganga Ghat (Confluence of 5 Rivers): Where 5 sacred rivers — Ganga, Yamuna, Saraswati, Kirana, and Dhutapapa — meet invisibly. The tapasthali of Sant Kabir, Shrimad Vallabhacharya, and Swami Ramananda. Site of the magnificent 17th-century Alamgir Mosque built over the ancient Bindu Madhav Vishnu Temple.
  • 5. Rajghat (The Northern Gateway): The northernmost historical ghat where Ancient Kashi began. Connected to Malaviya Bridge and Namo Ghat (featuring the famous 75-feet Namaste hand sculptures and helipad facility).

Complete Index of Famous Heritage Ghats from South to North:

Assi to Chet Singh Ghat: Assi Ghat, Ganga Mahal Ghat, Rewa Ghat, Tulsi Ghat (where Goswami Tulsidas composed Ramcharitmanas), Bhadaini Ghat, Anandamayi Ghat, Jain Ghat (birthplace of 7th Tirthankara Suparshvanatha), Nishadraj Ghat, and Chet Singh Ghat (fortified palace of Maharaja Chet Singh).
Shivala to Kedar Ghat: Shivala Ghat, Mahanirvani Ghat, Dandi Ghat, Hanuman Ghat (established by Lord Hanuman and Swami Ramdas), Lali Ghat, Vijayanagaram Ghat, Kedar Ghat (housing Sri Gauri Kedar Temple, a replica of Kedarnath Jyotirlinga).
Chowki to Dashashwamedh Ghat: Chauki Ghat, Kshemeshwar Ghat, Mansarovar Ghat, Pandey Ghat, Digpatia Ghat, Chousatti Ghat (dedicated to 64 Yoginis), Rana Mahal Ghat, Darbhanga Ghat (featuring the luxurious BrijRama Palace), Ahilyabai Ghat, Munshi Ghat, and Dashashwamedh Ghat.
Man Mandir to Manikarnika Ghat: Man Mandir Ghat (featuring Raja Man Singh Observatory & Jantar Mantar), Tripura Bhairavi Ghat, Mir Ghat, Phuta Ghat, Nepali Ghat (featuring wooden Pagoda-style Nepali Pashupatinath Temple), Lalita Ghat (entrance to Kashi Corridor), and Manikarnika Ghat.
Scindia to Namo Ghat: Scindia Ghat (housing the famous partially submerged Ratneshwar Mahadev Leaning Temple), Sankatha Ghat, Ganga Mahal Ghat, Bhosale Ghat, Panchganga Ghat, Durga Ghat, Brahma Ghat, Gai Ghat, Trilochan Ghat, Rajghat, and Namo Ghat.

10. Sacred Panchkroshi Yatra, Ashta Bhairava & Nav Durga Temples of Kashi

Beyond the main Kashi Vishwanath temple, classical Hindu scriptures describe the sacred 55-mile Panchkroshi Parikrama — a 5-day walking pilgrimage encircling 108 holy shrines around the outer perimeter of Kashi Dham.

The 5 Stops (Halts) of Panchkroshi Yatra:

  1. Kardameshwar Mahadev (Kandwa): The first halt housing a 12th-century stone temple built by the Gahadavala dynasty.
  2. Bhimchandi Devi (Bhimchandi): The second halt dedicated to Goddess Durga defeating demon Durgamasura.
  3. Rameshwar Mahadev (Rameshwar): Located on the banks of Varuna River, housing Shivalingas installed by Lord Ram.
  4. Shivpur (5 Shiv Shrines): The fourth halt featuring Sri Pancha Pandava Mandir.
  5. Kapildhara (Rajghat): The final halt where pilgrims complete ritual offerings before concluding at Manikarnika.

  • Chanda Bhairava: Durga Kund

11. Sarnath Buddhist Heritage Circuit & Archaeological Monuments

Located just 10 km northeast of Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Sarnath (Isipatana) is one of the four most sacred Buddhist pilgrimage destinations in the world. It is here in the Deer Park (Rishipatana) that Lord Buddha preached his first sermon after attaining enlightenment at Bodhgaya, turning the Wheel of Law (Dharmachakra Pravartana).

Major Monuments & Attractions in Sarnath:

Dhamek Stupa: Massive 43.6-meter tall cylindrical stone stupa built by Emperor Ashoka in 249 BCE to mark the exact spot of Buddha's first sermon. Features exquisite Gupta-era stone carvings.
Sarnath Archaeological Museum: Housing India's National Emblem — the original 3rd century BCE polished sandstone Ashoka Lion Capital, along with 5th century preaching Buddha statues.
Chaukhandi Stupa: An imposing 5th-century Gupta-period terraced brick monument topped with an octagonal Mughal tower constructed by Todar Mal's son Govardhan to commemorate Emperor Humayun's visit.
Mulagandha Kuti Vihar & Thai Temple: Modern Buddhist temple featuring colorful frescoes painted by Japanese artist Kosetsu Nosu, housing sacred Buddha relics, surrounded by international monasteries from Thailand, Japan, Tibet, and Sri Lanka.

12. Sawan Month Kanwar Yatra & Maha Shivratri Festival Guide

The holy month of Shravan (Sawan) and the auspicious festival of Maha Shivratri witness the highest influx of Shiva devotees (Kanwariyas) visiting Kashi Vishwanath Dham. Over 5 million pilgrims perform Jalabhishekam with holy Ganga water brought on foot from Sultanganj or Gaumukh.

13. Kashi Moksha & Ancestral Ritual Services (Pinda Daan & Asthi Visarjan)

In Hindu theology, performing ancestral rites (Shraddh Karma) in Kashi frees ancestors' souls from earthly bondage, granting them eternal peace and salvation. Kashi Dharshan provides certified Teerth Purohit (local Vedic Pandits) for performing sacred rituals at designated ghats.
