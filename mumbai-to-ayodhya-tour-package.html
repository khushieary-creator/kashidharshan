Mumbai to Ayodhya 3-Day Yatra Itinerary

Designed specifically for pilgrims arriving from Mumbai (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Airport / CSMT Station).

Day 1 — Mumbai Departure & Ayodhya Arrival

Direct Flight / Train Transit & Saryu Aarti

Board your morning flight from Mumbai (BOM) to Ayodhya Dham (AYJ) (approx 2 hours). On arrival, our private AC Sedan cab driver will greet you and transfer you to the hotel. In the evening, visit Saryu River Ghat to witness the grand Saryu Aarti and enjoy Ram Ki Paidi lights.

Day 2 — Hanuman Garhi & Ram Mandir VIP Darshan

Sacred Temple Walkthrough

Start with morning darshan at Hanuman Garhi temple. Proceed to the grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir using our pre-arranged VIP entry passes to bypass long queues. In the afternoon, visit Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and the quiet gardens of Mani Parvat.

Day 3 — Guptar Ghat Sightseeing & Return to Mumbai

Local Pilgrimage & Departure

Visit Nageshwarnath Temple and Guptar Ghat (where Lord Ram took Jal Samadhi). Check out from the hotel by noon. Our cab will drop you back at Ayodhya airport or station for your return journey to Mumbai.
