Chitrakoot Dham — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide

Complete spiritual & travel guide to Chitrakoot Dham. Learn about Kamadgiri Parikrama, Ram Ghat Aarti, Sphatik Shila, Hanuman Dhara & Gupt Godavari.

Chitrakoot Dham is the sacred forest abode where Lord Ram, Mata Sita, and Shri Laxman spent 11 and a half years of their 14-year exile (Ram Vanvas).

Surrounded by lush Vindhya hills and Mandakini River, Chitrakoot is renowned for Kamadgiri Parikrama and divine serene energy.

1. Kamadgiri Parikrama & Temple

The holy hill believed to be the embodiment of Lord Ram. Devotees perform 5 km barefoot parikrama around Kamadgiri to fulfill their deepest wishes.

2. Ram Ghat & Mandakini River Evening Aarti

The vibrant riverbank where Goswami Tulsidas had divine darshan of Lord Ram and Laxman. Evening Aarti on Mandakini river is deeply soul-stirring.

3. Sphatik Shila & Janaki Kund

Sphatik Shila holds the sacred footprints of Lord Ram and Sita Ji on a quartz rock. Janaki Kund is where Mata Sita bathed in Mandakini River.

4. Gupt Godavari & Hanuman Dhara

Mystic twin caves containing secret streams of Godavari River. Hanuman Dhara features a mountain waterfall cooling Lord Hanuman Ji after Lanka Dahan.
