Prayagraj (Triveni Sangam) — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide

Complete spiritual & travel guide to Prayagraj (Sangam). Learn about Triveni Sangam Snan, Bade Hanuman Mandir, Akshayavat, Alopi Devi & Kumbh Mela.

Prayagraj (Tirtharaj), the king of all holy pilgrimage centers, is where the sacred rivers Ganga, Yamuna, and the invisible Saraswati meet in divine union.

Whether you are seeking holy Sangam snan, ancient Pind Daan rituals, or Hanuman Ji darshan, this guide provides complete information on divine sights and heritage.

1. Triveni Sangam Holy Snan & Boat Trip

The sacred confluence of Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati. Taking a dip at the Sangam by private boat is believed to cleanse all sins and grant spiritual liberation.

2. Bade Hanuman Mandir (Lying Hanuman Ji)

The unique temple of Lord Hanuman in a reclining (sleeping) posture. During monsoon, holy Ganga water floods the temple to touch Hanuman Ji's lotus feet.

3. Ancient Akshayavat & Patalpuri Temple

The indestructible banyan tree inside Prayagraj Fort visited by Lord Ram, Sita, and Laxman during Ram Vanvas. Nearby Patalpuri temple houses ancient idols.

4. Alopi Devi Shakti Peeth & Anand Bhawan

Maa Alopi Devi is one of the 51 Shakti Peethas where only a wooden palanquin (doli) is worshipped. Anand Bhawan is the historic ancestral home of the Nehru family.
