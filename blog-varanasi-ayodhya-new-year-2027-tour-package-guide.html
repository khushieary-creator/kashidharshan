1. Why Choose a Spiritual Yatra for New Year 2027?

While millions spend New Year's Eve in crowded party destinations, a rapidly increasing number of families, elders, and young professionals choose to begin their New Year with sacred spiritual clarity. Kashi (Varanasi), the city of Lord Shiva that grants liberation, and Ayodhya, the city of Dharma and Maryada Purushottam Shri Ram, offer an unforgettable, soul-recharging start to 2027.

Standing on the illuminated banks of the holy River Ganga as bells chime for the evening Aarti, taking a dip in the purifying waters, and witnessing the golden spire of Kashi Vishwanath on the first morning of January sets a peaceful, prosperous vibration for the entire year.

2. Recommended 4-Day/3-Night New Year 2027 Itinerary

📅 Day 1 (30 December 2026): Arrival & Sarnath Peace Tour

• Airport/Railway pickup in private AC cab & check-in at hotel near Godowlia.
• Afternoon trip to sacred Sarnath (Dhamek Stupa & Deer Park where Lord Buddha taught).
• Evening orientation walk around local Banarasi silk markets.

🎉 Day 2 (31 December 2026): New Year's Eve Ganga Aarti & Ghats Cruise

• Morning visit to Kaal Bhairav Mandir (Kotwal of Kashi) and Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir.
• 04:30 PM: Board private luxury Bajra with warm blankets for 84 Ghats sunset cruise.
• 06:00 PM: Exclusive front-row river view of Dashashwamedh Ghat Ganga Aarti.
• Peaceful satvik New Year dinner at traditional Banarasi restaurant.

🛕 Day 3 (1 January 2027): First Darshan of 2027 & Drive to Ayodhya

• 06:30 AM: VIP Sugam Darshan at Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham & Maa Annapurna.
• Post-breakfast: Highway drive via Purvanchal route to Ayodhya (3.5 hours).
• Evening Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi & Hanuman Garhi darshan.

🚩 Day 4 (2 January 2027): Grand Ram Mandir Darshan & Departure

• Early morning VIP Darshan at the grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir.
• Visit Kanak Bhawan & Dashrath Mahal.
• Return cab transfer to Ayodhya Airport / Varanasi Airport for onward journey.
