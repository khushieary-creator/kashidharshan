Chitrakoot Dham — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide 2026

Ram Vanvas Guide4 min readPublished 2026
In this guide
  1. Kamadgiri Parikrama & Temple
  2. Ram Ghat & Mandakini River Aarti
  3. Sphatik Shila & Janaki Kund
  4. Gupt Godavari & Hanuman Dhara

1. Kamadgiri Parikrama & Temple

The holy hill believed to be the living form of Lord Ram. Devotees perform a 5 km barefoot parikrama around Kamadgiri to fulfill sacred vows.

2. Ram Ghat & Mandakini River Aarti

The famous riverbank where Goswami Tulsidas received divine darshan of Lord Ram. Evening Aarti on Mandakini River is filled with lamps and Vedic chants.

3. Sphatik Shila & Janaki Kund

Sphatik Shila holds the sacred footprints of Lord Ram and Sita Ji on a quartz rock. Janaki Kund is where Mata Sita bathed in Mandakini River.

4. Gupt Godavari & Hanuman Dhara

Mystic twin caves containing secret streams of Godavari River. Hanuman Dhara features a mountain waterfall cooling Lord Hanuman Ji.
