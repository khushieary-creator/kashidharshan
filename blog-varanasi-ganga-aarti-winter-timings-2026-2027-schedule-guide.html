Varanasi Ganga Aarti Winter Timings 2026-2027: Dashashwamedh, Assi & Namo Ghat Schedule

Critical seasonal timing update: As sunset advances in winter (November 2026 to February 2027), the world-famous evening Ganga Aarti shifts from 07:00 PM to 06:00 PM. Complete ghat schedule, boat booking arrival times, and essential woolen tips for pilgrims.

⚡

Direct Answer: What Time is Ganga Aarti in Varanasi Today (Winter Season)?

In the winter season (November 2026 to February 2027), the evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat begins promptly at 06:00 PM (sunset at ~05:25 PM) and concludes at 06:45 PM. At Assi Ghat, the evening Aarti starts at 06:15 PM, while morning Subah-e-Banaras begins at 05:30 AM. At Namo Ghat, the Aarti takes place at 06:30 PM.

⚠️ Critical Warning for Tourists: Hundreds of travelers arrive at 06:45 PM relying on outdated internet guides showing 07:00 PM summer timing and miss the Aarti completely. To secure prime riverfront seating or boat anchor spots, you must arrive at the ghat by 05:00 PM!

2. Detailed Ghat-by-Ghat Winter Aarti Experience

Dashashwamedh Ghat: The Grand Maha Aarti (Ganga Seva Nidhi)

Conducted by the Ganga Seva Nidhi trust, this is the iconic ceremony shown in global documentaries. Seven young priests dressed in saffron dhotis and kurtas conduct synchronized rituals on raised wooden platforms. The ceremony begins with the blowing of conch shells (Shankhanaad), lighting of incense sticks (Dhoop), waving of peacock-feather fans (Chamara), and culminating in the magnificent 108-wick brass lamps weighing 4.5 kg each.

Priests: 7 Batuks Winter Start: 06:00 PM Sharp Duration: 45 Minutes Best View: Anchored Bajra Boat

Assi Ghat: Peaceful Morning Hawan & Evening Chants

Assi Ghat offers a dual Aarti experience. In winter, Subah-e-Banaras begins at 05:30 AM with a sacred Vedic Yagya by young Brahmins from Panini Kanya Mahavidyalaya, followed by morning Aarti, morning classical sitar/flute recitals, and yoga as the red sun rises over the eastern mist. In the evening at 06:15 PM, a soothing five-priest Aarti takes place, ideal for travelers seeking peace without the intense crowds of Dashashwamedh.

Morning Aarti: 05:30 AM Evening Aarti: 06:15 PM Atmosphere: Meditative & Cultural

Namo Ghat: Modern Riverfront Promenade & Illuminated Namaste

Varanasi's northernmost developed ghat with giant folded-hands sculptures. In winter, the evening Aarti takes place at 06:30 PM against the scenic backdrop of the Malviya (Rajghat) Bridge. Namo Ghat is uniquely equipped with dedicated car parking, accessible ramps, clean restrooms, and open food plazas—making it the most convenient option for families with wheelchairs or strollers.

Winter Start: 06:30 PM Accessibility: 100% Barrier-Free Parking: On-Site AC Cab Parking

4. 5 Winter Pro-Tips for Attending Varanasi Ganga Aarti

🧥 Riverfront Wind Chill Factor

Even if the city temperature reads 18°C, sitting in an open boat on the Ganges creates a wind chill feeling like 12°C. Carry a woolen cap, shawl, or jacket, especially for kids and elderly parents.

🚦 Godowlia No-Vehicle Zone

In winter, Varanasi traffic police bar 4-wheelers beyond Godowlia / Luxa crossing from 03:00 PM to 09:00 PM. Get dropped at Godowlia and walk 5 minutes or hire a cycle-rickshaw (₹30) to Dashashwamedh Ghat.

🥛 Savor Winter Malaiyo Post-Aarti

Immediately after Aarti finishes at 06:45 PM, walk into the Godowlia-Chowk galis to taste Malaiyo—Varanasi's legendary winter-only saffron-pistachio dew-drop milk foam served in earthen kulhads (₹40–₹60).

Frequently Asked Questions (Ganga Aarti Winter FAQs)

What time does Ganga Aarti start in Varanasi in winter?

In winter (November to February), the evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat starts at 06:00 PM and concludes at 06:45 PM. At Assi Ghat, the evening Aarti begins at 06:15 PM, while the morning Subah-e-Banaras Aarti starts at 05:30 AM.

Why does Varanasi Ganga Aarti timing change in winter?

The ceremony is synchronized with dusk (sunset). In summer, the sun sets around 06:45 PM–07:00 PM, scheduling Aarti at 07:00 PM. In winter, sunset advances to 05:15 PM–05:30 PM, shifting the Aarti start to 06:00 PM.

What time should I reach or board a boat for winter evening Aarti?

You should reach Dashashwamedh Ghat by 05:00 PM and board your boat by 05:15 PM. Boats anchoring after 05:40 PM get stuck in the 3rd or 4th row where views of the priests and fire lamps are largely obstructed.

Is there any entry ticket for winter Ganga Aarti?

Attending the Aarti on the ghat stone steps is 100% free and open to everyone. If you wish to sit on a private boat on the river, prices range from ₹1,200 to ₹1,800 for private row boats, ₹2,500 to ₹3,500 for motorboats, and ₹250 to ₹400 for shared bajra seats.

Which ghat is best to watch Ganga Aarti in Varanasi during winter?

Dashashwamedh Ghat is the premier choice for sheer spiritual grandeur. For peace and classical music, choose Assi Ghat. For elderly pilgrims requiring wheelchair access and easy car parking, Namo Ghat is best.
