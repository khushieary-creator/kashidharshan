Overview of Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor

The **Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor** is a monumental 5-Lakh Sq. Ft. spiritual mega-structure built to connect the ancient Jyotirlinga temple directly with the sacred Ganga river at Lalita Ghat. Pilgrims can now take a holy dip in Mother Ganga, collect Gangajal, and walk straight into Lord Shiva's sanctum through grand marble paved pathways lined with heritage buildings, museum halls, and Vedic libraries.

Locker Rules & Prohibited Items Inside Corridor

  • 📱 Prohibited Items: Mobile phones, cameras, smartwatches, leather belts, wallets, and bags are not permitted inside the Garbhagriha.
  • 🔐 Locker Service: Free digital lockers are located right at Gate No. 4 (Godowlia) and Lalita Ghat entrance. Token keys are issued free of charge.

Facilities for Senior Citizens & Differently-Abled Pilgrims

Navigating the corridor is smooth and effortless for elderly family members:

  • ⚡ Electric Golf Carts: Free golf carts transport senior citizens from Godowlia Gate No. 4 directly to the inner corridor.
  • ♿ Wheelchair Ramps & Lifts: Ramps and heavy-duty elevators are installed at Lalita Ghat riverfront to reach the temple plaza easily.
  • 🏨 Near-Corridor Stays: Kashi Dharshan books hotels within 200 meters of Gate No. 4 for zero walking stress.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. How far is Kashi Vishwanath Corridor from Varanasi Railway Station (BSB)?

Varanasi Junction (BSB) is ~4.5 km from Gate No. 4 (Godowlia Crossing), taking around 15 minutes by auto or AC cab.

Q2. What is the distance between Lalita Ghat and Kashi Vishwanath Temple?

Lalita Ghat is directly connected to the temple precinct inside the corridor via a 300-meter paved marble pathway with escalators.
