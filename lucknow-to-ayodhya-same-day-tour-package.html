Same-Day Yatra Itinerary & Timeline

A tight, seamless 12-hour yatra schedule designed to avoid queues and maximize comfort.

07:00 AM — Lucknow Pickup

Morning Drive to Ayodhya

Our professional English/Hindi speaking driver will pick you up in a sanitized AC Sedan/SUV from Lucknow Airport (LKO), Charbagh Station, or your residence. Travel comfortably via NH 28 highway (135 km).

10:00 AM — Ayodhya Arrival & Hanuman Garhi

Lord Hanuman Prayers

On arrival, meet our local coordinator who will guide you to Hanuman Garhi. Seek blessings at this sacred fort temple, custom-guided to help senior citizens navigate stairs comfortably.

11:30 AM — Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Darshan

VIP Entry Assistance

Proceed directly to the grand Ram Mandir. Using pre-arranged priority passes (Sugam Darshan), skip public crowds to complete your prayers comfortably. Explore the temple architecture.

02:00 PM — Sattvic Lunch & Kanak Bhawan

Lunch & Local Sightseeing

Enjoy a delicious, pure vegetarian Sattvic lunch. Post lunch, visit Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and Ram Ki Paidi ghats.

05:30 PM — Return Journey to Lucknow

Departure Transfer

Board your private cab for the return drive. Arrive back in Lucknow by 8:30 PM for airport/station drop-offs.
