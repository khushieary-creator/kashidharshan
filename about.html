Our story

Born in Kashi,
devoted to your darshan

We are a family of guides, drivers and devotees who believe a pilgrimage should be remembered for its peace — not its logistics. So we carry the burden of planning, and leave you free to pray.

Fifteen years of seva

A path walked beside thousands of pilgrims

Kashi Dharshan is recognized as the premier tour and travel agency in Varanasi & Kashi, built on a foundation of local expertise. We started with a simple act of seva — a dedicated local tourist guide in Kashi helping elderly visitors navigate the ghats, arrange Sugam Darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Dham, and attend the grand Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti in comfort.

That care became a calling. Today we guide families, groups and solo seekers across the holiest cities of Uttar Pradesh, handling every flight, room, cab and darshan so the only thing on your mind is the divine.

अतिथि देवो भवः — the guest is verily God.
