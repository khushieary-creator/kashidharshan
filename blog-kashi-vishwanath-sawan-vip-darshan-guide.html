During the holy month of Shravan (Sawan), Varanasi transforms into a sea of saffron as lakhs of Kanwariyas and devotees arrive to offer holy Ganga water to Lord Shiva. The newly built **Kashi Vishwanath Corridor** has made it easier to handle large volumes, but on key days—especially **Sawan Mondays (Somvars)**—the crowd count routinely crosses 3,00,000. Standing in ordinary queues under these conditions can take anywhere from 3 to 6 hours. To save time and experience a comfortable pilgrimage, securing a Kashi VIP Darshan (Sugam Darshan) pass is highly recommended.

The Sawan Rush at Kashi Vishwanath

Sawan is considered the most sacred month for Shiva worship. Devotees walk hundreds of kilometers fetching water from the Ganga in Sultanganj or Haridwar, walking all the way to Varanasi. On weekdays, queue times are moderately long, but on Mondays, the temple remains packed 24/7. For families, senior citizens, and young children, standing in these long, slow-moving barricades is highly exhausting. Securing priority entry is the best way to ensure safety and comfort.

What is Sugam Darshan (VIP Entry)?

The Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust offers a fast-track queue system called **Sugam Darshan**. A Sugam Darshan ticket gives you:

  • Access to a dedicated priority gate and queue lane, completely bypassing the general public crowd blocks.
  • A dedicated temple priest (Shastri) who escorts you inside the sanctum sanctorum (Garbhagriha) to perform your prayers smoothly.
  • Prasad and a protective sacred thread (Kalava) blessed at the temple.
  • Overall time spent inside the corridor drops to under 20-30 minutes.

Steps to Book Passes Online

To book your priority darshan passes directly from the temple trust, follow this official process:

  1. Go to the official temple portal: shrikashivishwanath.org.
  2. Navigate to the **"Online Services"** section and select **"Sugam Darshan"** or **"Aarti"**.
  3. Choose your preferred date, reporting slot time, and fill in details of all visitors (Aadhaar or Passport ID details required).
  4. Pay the transaction fee online.
  5. Download and print your barcode receipt. You must show the barcode receipt and matching ID card at the entry gate security kiosk.

💡 Tip: Sawan Monday slots are extremely limited and book out up to 30 days in advance. Ensure you log in early to secure your desired dates.
