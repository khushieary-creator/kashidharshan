Kashi Darshan 2026: Top 10 Places, 1-Day & 2-Day Itinerary, Timings & Tour Cost

Everything you need to know for planning a divine, hassle-free Kashi Darshan yatra. Complete list of top 10 sacred places to visit, hour-by-hour 1-day and 2-day itineraries, temple darshan timings, taxi rates, and local pandit guide tips.

Quick Overview: Planning Your Kashi Darshan in 2026

Kashi (Varanasi / Banaras) is the timeless city of Lord Shiva where the physical and metaphysical worlds converge. Whether arriving by train at Varanasi Cantt (BSB) / Banaras (BSBS) or flight at Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS), having a structured itinerary saves hours of confusion amidst Varanasi's ancient narrow galis.

⏱️ Ideal Duration: 1 to 2 Days (3 Days if including Ayodhya/Prayagraj)
📍 Core Hub: Godowlia / Dashashwamedh Ghat
🚗 Best Transit: AC Cab + Walking in Galis
🕉️ Mandatory Darshan: Vishwanath Dham + Kaal Bhairav

1. Top 10 Sacred Places to Visit in Kashi Darshan

When embarking on a sacred pilgrimage to Kashi, these 10 divine landmarks form the spiritual soul of Banaras:

1. Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham (Golden Temple)

The spiritual centerpiece of Hinduism housing one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas. The newly transformed 5-lakh sq. ft. corridor directly links Manikarnika and Lalita Ghats on River Ganga to the sanctum sanctorum. Timings: 03:00 AM to 11:00 PM. VIP Sugam Darshan recommended.

2. Baba Kaal Bhairav Mandir (Kotwal of Kashi)

Known as the divine Police Chief of Kashi. According to local scriptures, your Kashi yatra remains incomplete without offering prayers to Baba Kaal Bhairav to receive permission to enter and leave the city safely. Mustard oil lamps and sacred black protective threads (Kashi Ka Ganda) are offered here.

3. Maa Annapurna Mandir

Located just 50 meters from Kashi Vishwanath sanctum. Maa Annapurna is the Goddess of nourishment who fed Lord Shiva himself. A sacred silver idol is worshipped daily, and during Annakoot Mahotsav (Diwali week), the golden idol is opened for darshan.

4. Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir

Established by saint Goswami Tulsidas at the exact spot where he had the divine vision of Lord Hanuman. The temple is famous for overcoming planetary obstacles (especially Shani dosha) and offers the world-famous pure desi ghee Besan Ladoo prasad.

5. Dashashwamedh Ghat & Evening Maha Ganga Aarti

The most vibrant ghat in Varanasi where Lord Brahma performed ten Ashwamedha yagyas. Every evening at sunset (06:30 PM), seven young priests perform the synchronized Maha Aarti with brass multi-tiered lamps, conch shells, and rhythmic chants viewed by thousands from riverfront bajra boats.

6. Assi Ghat (Subah-e-Banaras Dawn Experience)

The southernmost ghat where the holy river Assi meets Mother Ganga. Host to the world-renowned Subah-e-Banaras at 05:00 AM, featuring Vedic yagya, sunrise Ganga Aarti, morning classical sitar/flute ragas, open yoga, and the departure point for sunrise boat rides.

7. Manikarnika Ghat (The Mahashmashan)

The eternal cremation ground where funeral pyres burn continuously 24×7. According to belief, Lord Shiva personally whispers the Taraka Mantra into the ears of those cremated here, liberating their souls from the cycle of rebirth. Pilgrims observe the philosophical reality of life from respectful boat viewpoints.

8. New Vishwanath Temple (VT) at BHU

Built by the Birla family inside the lush 1,300-acre Banaras Hindu University campus. It boasts the tallest temple tower (shikhara) in the world (77 meters) made of pristine white marble with verses of the Bhagavad Gita carved into its walls.

9. Maa Durga Kund & Tulsi Manas Mandir

The iconic 18th-century red stone Durga Kund Mandir, dedicated to Goddess Kushmanda. Next to it stands Tulsi Manas Mandir, built at the very site where Tulsidas composed the Ramcharitmanas, featuring illuminated mechanical scenes depicting the epic.

10. Sarnath Sacred Buddhist Circuit (10 km from Kashi)

Where Gautama Buddha delivered his first sermon (Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta) after enlightenment. Explore the colossal 5th-century Dhamek Stupa, Ashoka Pillar Lion Capital (India's national emblem in Archaeological Museum), and Thai/Japanese Buddhist monasteries.

2. 1-Day Express Kashi Darshan Itinerary (Hour-by-Hour)

If you have only 24 hours in Varanasi, follow this meticulously tested schedule to cover all primary shrines without missing the Ganga Aartis:

05:00 AM – 07:00 AM
Subah-e-Banaras & Sunrise Boat Tour: Arrive at Assi Ghat for Vedic Hawan and morning Aarti. Board a wooden row boat or motorboat to witness the ghats bathed in golden morning light up to Manikarnika Ghat.
07:30 AM – 08:30 AM
Banarasi Breakfast Trail: Relish piping-hot Bedmi Kachori with Sabzi and crisp saffron Jalebi at Ram Bhandar (Thatheri Bazaar) or Netaji Chachi (Assi). Top it off with kulhad chai.
09:00 AM – 11:30 AM
Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham & Maa Annapurna: Enter via Gate 4 (Godowlia side). With a pre-booked VIP Sugam Darshan Pass, complete abhishekam and darshan within 45 minutes. Visit Maa Annapurna next door.
12:00 PM – 01:15 PM
Baba Kaal Bhairav Mandir: Take an e-rickshaw to Visheshwarganj to seek the blessings of the Kotwal of Kashi. Offer sacred mustard oil and tie the sacred black thread.
01:30 PM – 02:45 PM
Satvik Banarasi Thali Lunch: Savor a pure vegetarian lunch (Satvik Dal, Baati Chokha, Paneer & Kheer) at Godowlia or Sigra restaurants.
03:15 PM – 05:30 PM
Sarnath Excursion: Board your AC cab to Sarnath. Explore Dhamek Stupa, Mulagandha Kuti Vihar, and the Archaeological Museum showcasing the Ashoka Lion Capital.
06:15 PM – 08:00 PM
Grand Evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh: Pre-board your reserved riverfront Bajra boat by 06:15 PM to secure front-row views of the spellbinding Maha Aarti. Conclude your evening with authentic Banarasi Paan.

3. 2-Day Complete Kashi Darshan Itinerary (Recommended)

A 2-day yatra provides a relaxed, unhurried pilgrimage—ideal for families with children and senior citizens:

Day 1: Sacred Riverfront & Core Jyotirlinga

DAY 1
  • 05:30 AM: Sunrise Subah-e-Banaras at Assi Ghat + Morning wooden boat ride.
  • 08:00 AM: Authentic breakfast (Kachori, Jalebi & Lassi).
  • 09:30 AM: Priority VIP Darshan at Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham & Maa Annapurna.
  • 12:00 PM: Darshan at Baba Kaal Bhairav & Maa Vishalakshi Devi (Shaktipeeth).
  • 01:30 PM: Traditional lunch & brief hotel rest during afternoon heat.
  • 05:30 PM: Heritage walking tour through narrow Vishwanath corridor galis.
  • 06:30 PM: Reserved front-row boat seating for Dashashwamedh Evening Maha Aarti.
  • 08:30 PM: Godowlia market walk & winter Malaiyo dessert tasting.

Day 2: City Temples, Silk Handloom & Sarnath

DAY 2
  • 07:30 AM: Visit Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple (Besan Ladoo prasad).
  • 08:45 AM: Darshan at Durga Kund Mandir, Tulsi Manas, and Tridev Temple.
  • 10:15 AM: Tour of Banaras Hindu University (BHU) & New Vishwanath Temple (VT).
  • 11:30 AM: Authentic Banarasi Silk Saree handloom weaving visit at Pilikothi.
  • 01:00 PM: Lunch at a premier multi-cuisine restaurant.
  • 02:30 PM: Sarnath Buddhist excursion (Dhamek Stupa, Bodhi Tree & Museum).
  • 05:30 PM: Visit Namo Ghat to view the iconic folded-hands sculpture.
  • 07:00 PM: Evening departure transfer to Varanasi Airport or Railway Station.

5. Essential Temple Rules, Dress Codes & Senior Citizen Tips

📱 Electronics & Mobile Phone Ban

Mobile phones, electronic smartwatches, leather belts, and large bags are strictly prohibited inside the inner sanctum of Kashi Vishwanath. Verified electronic locker facilities are available at Gate 4 and Corridor Help Desks for ₹20–₹50.

👗 Sacred Dress Code Guidelines

For general darshan, decent traditional clothing (Kurta-Pyjama or formal trousers for men; Sarees or Salwar suits for women) is recommended. If performing Sparsh Darshan during early morning hours, men must wear a traditional Dhoti and women must wear a Saree.

🦽 Wheelchair & Golf Cart Facility

For elderly pilgrims, battery-operated golf carts operate from Gate 4 right up to the temple chowk. Clean wheelchairs with trained temple assistants can be requested at the help desk free of charge.

Frequently Asked Questions (Kashi Darshan FAQs)

How many days are needed for a complete Kashi Darshan?

2 Days and 1 Night is the ideal duration. Day 1 covers the sacred riverfront (Assi morning aarti, Vishwanath Dham, Kaal Bhairav, Annapurna, and evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh). Day 2 covers Sarnath Buddhist stupas, BHU New Vishwanath Temple, Sankat Mochan, and Durga Mandir.

What is the correct sequence of temples to visit in Kashi Darshan?

According to Kashi Khand and local tradition, pilgrims first take a holy dip in the Ganges, visit Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham & Maa Annapurna, followed mandatorily by Baba Kaal Bhairav (Kotwal of Kashi, whose permission is essential to conclude the yatra). Then visit Maa Vishalakshi, Sankat Mochan Hanuman, and Durga Mandir.

How much does a 1-day Kashi Darshan tour package cost?

A standard 1-day Kashi Darshan tour with private AC cab (Dzire/Ertiga) costs ₹2,500 to ₹3,500 for vehicle rental (8 hours/80 km). Complete guided packages including VIP Sugam Darshan pass assistance and private boat ride start at ₹2,900 to ₹4,999 per couple or family group.

Can senior citizens comfortably do Kashi Darshan?

Yes. Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham has battery-operated golf carts and wheelchair ramps from Gate No. 4 (Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Gate / Godowlia side). Booking a VIP Sugam Darshan pass is strongly advised to bypass 2 to 3 hour general waiting queues.

What are the opening timings of Kashi Vishwanath Temple?

Kashi Vishwanath Temple opens at 03:00 AM for Mangla Aarti and closes at 11:00 PM after Shayan Aarti. General darshan is available between 04:00 AM to 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM to 07:00 PM, and 08:30 PM to 11:00 PM.

Can we visit Sarnath and Kashi temples in a single day?

Yes. An early 05:00 AM start allows covering Assi Ghat, Vishwanath Temple, and Kaal Bhairav by 12:30 PM. After lunch, take a 30-minute private cab ride to Sarnath (10 km away) to explore Dhamek Stupa and Museum from 02:30 PM to 05:00 PM, returning in time for the 06:30 PM Dashashwamedh Ganga Aarti.
