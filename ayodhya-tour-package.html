Ayodhya Tour Package 2026Ram Mandir VIP Pass, Hanuman Garhi & Saryu Aarti

An unhurried, spiritual yatra exploring the ancient temples of Lord Ram's holy birthplace. Ask for our specialized ayodhya chhapaiya tour package for Swaminarayan temple visits. VIP darshan assistance, comfortable accommodations close to temples, and private local travel are fully included.

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Welcome & Sunset Saryu Aarti

Arrival & Ram ki Paidi

Arrive in Ayodhya. Meet our guide and driver, transfer to your hotel. In the evening, visit Ram ki Paidi for the beautiful sunset Saryu River Aarti.

Day 2 — Core Darshan Day

Hanuman Garhi & Ram Janmabhoomi

Begin early by seeking blessings at Hanuman Garhi, followed by priority darshan at the magnificent Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir. In the afternoon, visit Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and the ancient Nageshwarnath Temple.

Day 3 — Holy Snan & Departure

Guptar Ghat & Farewell

Start with a sunrise holy dip in the Saryu River, visit Guptar Ghat (where Lord Ram took Jal Samadhi), and explore local markets before your departure transfer.

Senior Citizen & Wheelchair Care Services

We specialize in making pilgrimages comfortable for elderly parents and disabled devotees. Our dedicated care services ensure peace of mind:

  • Wheelchair Assistance: Pre-arranged wheelchair escorts at Ram Mandir and Kashi Vishwanath.
  • Temple-Side Drop-offs: Direct vehicle access drops as close to the temple entry gates as local rules permit.
  • Dedicated Yatra Guide: Patient, local guides who manage queue entries and security checks comfortably.
  • Comfort Stays & Cars: Hotels with lift access, ground floor rooms, and spacious AC SUVs (Ertiga/Innova).

How early should we book the Ayodhya package?+
