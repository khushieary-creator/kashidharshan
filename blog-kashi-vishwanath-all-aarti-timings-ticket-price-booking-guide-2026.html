Experience the Divine Energy of Baba Vishwanath

Witnessing an Aarti inside Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham is one of the most spiritually powerful experiences on earth. Every day, exactly five major Aartis are performed by the Vedic archakas, accompanied by damru beats, shankh naad, and sacred mantras.

Due to strictly capped seating inside the inner sanctum (Garbhagriha), Aarti passes fill up 30 to 45 days in advance. Below is the updated 2026 timetable, ticket charges, gate protocols, and booking secrets to ensure you never miss your darshan.

2. Detailed Significance of the 5 Daily Aartis

1. Mangla Aarti (03:00 AM – 04:00 AM) — The Dawn Awakening

Mangla Aarti is considered the crown jewel of Varanasi pilgrimages. Conducted during the sacred Brahma Muhurta, the priests unlock the Garbhagriha, awaken Lord Shiva with Vedic chants, bathe the Jyotirlinga with Panchamrit (milk, curd, honey, ghee, and sugar), and apply sandalwood paste. Devotees sitting inside the sanctum experience electrifying vibrations as damrus thunder and conch shells resonate. Read our dedicated Mangla Aarti Detailed Guide for specialized booking tips.

2. Bhog Aarti (11:15 AM – 12:20 PM) — The Royal Midday Feast

At midday, Baba Vishwanath is offered a grand royal feast (*Rajbhog*) consisting of pure sattvic delicacies cooked in the temple rasoi. The doors are briefly closed during the offering, followed by a majestic aarti with camphor flame towers. This aarti is ideal for families with children and elderly pilgrims who cannot wake up at 2:00 AM for Mangla Aarti.

3. Sandhya Aarti (07:00 PM – 08:15 PM) — Twilight Deepam & Damru Aarti

As twilight descends on the Ganga, the Sandhya Aarti takes place simultaneously with the grand Dashashwamedh Ghat Ganga Aarti. During Sandhya Aarti, the Jyotirlinga is adorned with bilva leaves and fresh flowers, surrounded by grand brass lamps. It is one of the most musically captivating rituals in Kashi.

4. Shringar Bhog Aarti (09:00 PM – 10:15 PM) — Divine Floral Adornment

During Shringar Aarti, Lord Shiva is elaborately decorated like a majestic bridegroom. The Jyotirlinga is crowned with silver and gold crowns, adorned with marigold garlands, lotus blossoms, and anointed with natural scents (*Itr*). If you love visual grandeur and devotional hymns, Shringar Aarti is unmissable.

5. Shayan Aarti (10:30 PM – 11:00 PM) — Sacred Night Rest

The final ritual before the temple closes for the night. The priests softly sing soothing lullabies (*Lori*) to Lord Shiva and Mother Parvati. A silver cot is placed, and a sacred game of Chaupar is arranged for the deities. The ambiance is exceptionally peaceful and serene.

3. How to Book Kashi Vishwanath Aarti Passes Online (Step-by-Step)

  1. Visit the Official Portal: Go to the official website: https://shrikashivishwanath.org or download the official mobile app.
  2. User Registration: Create an account using your mobile number and verify via OTP.
  3. Select Aarti Service: Navigate to the Aarti Services tab and choose between Mangla, Bhog, Sandhya, or Shringar Aarti.
  4. Choose Travel Date: Check the availability calendar. Green indicates available slots; red denotes sold out. Quota opens 45 days in advance at midnight (00:00 AM).
  5. Enter Devotee Particulars: Provide full name, age, gender, and government ID details (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport). The name on the ticket MUST exactly match your physical ID card!
  6. Payment & Pass Download: Complete the transaction using UPI, Credit Card, or Net Banking. Download and print the PDF pass with QR code.

4. Mandatory Dress Code & Security Protocol

👗 Required Clothing

  • Men: Unstitched traditional Dhoti and Kurta (white, saffron, or yellow). In Garbhagriha for Sparsh, upper body must be bare or draped with an angavastram.
  • Women: Traditional Indian Saree or Salwar Suit with Dupatta. Western wear or skirts are not permitted inside sanctum during Aarti.

🚫 Strictly Forbidden Items

  • Mobile phones, smartwatches & wireless earbuds.
  • Leather items (belts, wallets, shoes, leather watch straps).
  • Metal objects, scissors, matchboxes & lighters.
  • Tip: Free electronic lockers are provided outside Gate 2 & Gate 4.

6. Frequently Asked Questions (Pilgrim FAQs)

Can children attend Mangla Aarti?
Children below 5 years do not require a separate ticket, but they must remain seated with their parents. Children above 5 years need a full-fare ticket. Please ensure children stay quiet during solemn Vedic chants.
What happens if online Aarti passes are sold out?
If online passes are sold out on shrikashivishwanath.org, you can opt for Sugam Darshan VIP Pass (₹300) for priority temple entry, or approach our local yatra assistance helpline at +91 70119 60307 for authorized assistance.
Is photography permitted during Aarti?
No. Photography and videography are strictly prohibited inside the Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham complex. Electronic devices must be deposited in lockers outside.
Can senior citizens get wheelchair assistance for Aarti?
Yes! Wheelchairs and battery-operated golf carts are available from Gate No. 4 (Chhatta Dwar) and the Ganga View Gallery riverfront entrance for senior citizens and differently-abled devotees.
