Ayodhya Prayagraj Chitrakoot Varanasi Tour

Follow the divine footprints of Lord Ram's life journey. From his birthplace in Ayodhya, through the sacred confluence at Prayagraj, into the dense forests of his exile in Chitrakoot, ending at the eternal city of Varanasi.

6-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Welcome to Ayodhya

Ayodhya Arrival & Saryu Aarti

Arrive at Ayodhya airport or station and check in to your hotel. Spend a peaceful evening at Ram ki Paidi witnessing the magnificent sunset Saryu River Aarti.

Day 2 — Ayodhya Temple Darshan

Ram Janmabhoomi & Hanuman Garhi

Visit Hanuman Garhi temple and the grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir. In the afternoon, explore Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and other historical sites.

Day 3 — Prayagraj Sangam Snan

Triveni Sangam & Local Temples

Check out and drive to Prayagraj (approx. 3.5 hours). Board a private boat to Triveni Sangam for a holy dip. Visit Bade Hanuman Temple, Akshayavat, and Alopi Devi Shaktipeeth. Overnight in Prayagraj.

Day 4 — Chitrakoot Exile Trails & Transfer to Kashi

Ramghat, Kamadgiri & Drive to Varanasi

Drive to Chitrakoot (approx. 2.5 hours). Experience the peaceful Mandakini River at Ramghat, do parikrama at Kamadgiri, and explore Sphatik Shila and Gupt Godavari caves. In the evening, drive to Varanasi (approx. 4.5 hours) and check in to your hotel.

Day 5 — Kashi Vishwanath & Ganga Aarti

Varanasi Core Darshan & Ganga Boat Ride

Seek blessings at Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Annapurna Mandir, and Kal Bhairav Temple. In the evening, witness the divine Ganga Aarti from a private boat at Dashashwamedh Ghat.

Day 6 — Sunrise Boat Ride & Departure

Subah-e-Banaras & Sarnath

Experience a quiet sunrise boat ride along the historic ghats. Visit Sarnath (Dhamekh Stupa) before transferring to Varanasi airport or railway station for your onward journey.

FAQ — 4-City Tour

Is Chitrakoot visited in a single day?+
Yes, we explore the key spiritual spots of Chitrakoot (Ramghat, Kamadgiri, Sphatik Shila, and Gupt Godavari) during the transit journey between Prayagraj and Varanasi, keeping the schedule active yet comfortable.
Are senior citizen facilities available for this long tour?+
Absolutely. We provide options for wheelchair assistance, ground-floor hotel rooms, and slow-paced temple darshans tailored specifically for elderly pilgrims.
