Kashi Vishwanath Mangla Aarti Online Booking 2026: Pass Price, Timings & Entry Rules

Attending the divine Mangla Aarti at 03:00 AM inside Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple is the ultimate spiritual awakening experience in Kashi. Because only 250 to 300 seats are available daily inside the Garbhagriha, passes get fully sold out weeks in advance. Here is your definitive guide to booking tickets, entry gates, dress codes, and VIP yatra assistance.

1. What is Kashi Vishwanath Mangla Aarti?

Mangla Aarti is the very first ritual of the day performed to awaken Lord Shiva (Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga). The temple priests perform Vedic chanting, damru drumming, and offer sacred snan and floral shringara to the Jyotirlinga. Devotees sitting inside the Garbhagriha witness the divine energy from just a few feet away.

2. Official Ticket Price & Booking Timings (2026 Updated)

  • Aarti Timing: 03:00 AM to 04:00 AM daily.
  • Reporting Time: 02:15 AM to 02:45 AM at Gate No. 2 (Saraswati Gate via Vishwanath Gali).
  • Official Ticket Fee: ₹500 per person (Normal Days) | ₹1,000 – ₹2,000 (Sawan Mondays & Festival Days).
  • Advance Booking Window: Slots open 45 days prior on the official Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust portal (shrikashivishwanath.org).

3. Mandatory Dress Code & Prohibited Items

To enter the Garbhagriha during Mangla Aarti, strict traditional dress protocol is enforced by temple guards:

  • For Men: Unstitched traditional Dhoti and Kurta (Leather belts, trousers, jeans, or shorts are strictly forbidden).
  • For Women: Traditional Indian Saree or Salwar Kameez with Dupatta.
  • Strictly Prohibited: Mobile phones, electronic watches, digital keys, leather wallets, camera, and large bags. Deposit all items in the free locker counter at Gate 2.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Can children join Mangla Aarti?

Children above 5 years of age require a separate full ticket. Children below 5 years can accompany parents free of charge.

Q: What happens if I reach after 02:45 AM?

Entry gates close strictly at 02:45 AM for security screening and Vedic alignment. Late arrivals are denied entry without refund.

Q: Where should I stay for Mangla Aarti?

Stay at hotels located in Godowlia, Dashashwamedh Ghat or Bansphatak so you can easily walk to Gate 2 at 02:15 AM without needing autos.
