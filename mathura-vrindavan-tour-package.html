Mathura Vrindavan Tour

Explore the sacred lands of Braj Bhoomi. Experience the birth site of Lord Krishna in Mathura, witness the ecstatic devotion at Banke Bihari temple in Vrindavan, and perform the sacred parikrama at Govardhan Hill.

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrive in Mathura & Yamuna Aarti

Krishna Janmabhoomi & Dwarkadhish Mandir

On arrival at Delhi airport/station or Mathura station, transfer to your hotel. Visit the holy Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi (birthplace temple) and Dwarkadhish Mandir. In the evening, attend the beautiful Yamuna River Aarti at Vishram Ghat.

Day 2 — Vrindavan Spiritual Exploration

Banke Bihari Mandir & Prem Mandir

Spend a blissful day in Vrindavan. Seek blessings at Banke Bihari Temple, Radha Raman Temple, and ISKCON Temple. In the evening, witness the breathtaking illumination and musical fountain show at the Prem Mandir.

Day 3 — Govardhan Hill & Departure

Govardhan, Radha Kund & Barsana

Visit Govardhan Hill, Kusum Sarovar, and Radha Kund. In the afternoon, pay a visit to Barsana (Radha Rani Temple) before departing to Delhi or Mathura station for your onward journey.

Good to know

FAQ — Mathura Vrindavan

Are electronic rickshaws included in the package?+
Since outside vehicles are banned inside the narrow streets of Vrindavan near the Banke Bihari Temple, local e-rickshaws are the primary mode of travel. They are fully included in our Standard and Premium comfort tiers.
Can we do the Govardhan Parikrama by battery vehicle?+
Yes. If you prefer not to walk the 21 km parikrama path, we can organize a private battery operated cart or vehicle for the Govardhan Parikrama on request.
