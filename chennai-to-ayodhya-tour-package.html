Chennai to Ayodhya 4-Day Yatra Itinerary

Tailored specifically for pilgrims departing from Chennai International Airport (MAA) or Chennai Central / Egmore stations.

Day 1 — Chennai Departure & Saryu Aarti

Transit & Check-in

Depart from Chennai (MAA) via direct flight (2.5 hours) or AC Express train. Upon arrival, our private AC Sedan cab driver will transfer you to your hotel. Spend your evening attending the soulful Saryu Aarti and visiting Ram Ki Paidi.

Day 2 — Ram Janmabhoomi VIP Darshan

Hanuman Garhi & Ram Mandir

Visit Hanuman Garhi temple in the morning. Proceed for VIP Darshan at the grand Shri Ram Mandir (VIP entry passes included). Spend the afternoon exploring Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and Mani Parvat.

Day 3 — Local Sightseeing & Guptar Ghat

Nageshwarnath Temple & Jal Samadhi Sthal

Enjoy local sightseeing including Nageshwarnath Temple, Ram Katha Museum, and Guptar Ghat (where Lord Ram took Jal Samadhi). Option to coordinate pure South Indian meals/dining at local pure vegetarian venues.

Day 4 — Farewell & Departure to Chennai

Return Transit

Check out from the hotel after breakfast. The private cab driver will drop you at Ayodhya Airport or Station for your return flight/train back to Chennai.
