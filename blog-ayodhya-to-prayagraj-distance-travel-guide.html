Many devotees planning a trip to the newly built Ram Janmabhoomi temple in Ayodhya also seek to visit **Triveni Sangam** in Prayagraj (Allahabad). Connecting these two historic cities of Uttar Pradesh is smooth and easy. With an average road journey of just under 4 hours, a combined trip is highly recommended. This detailed guide covers distance, highway route conditions, direct trains, private taxi booking fares, and transport timings.

Ayodhya to Prayagraj Distance & Road Highway Route

The total road distance between Ayodhya and Prayagraj is approximately **170 kilometers (105 miles)**. The direct and most popular route is via **NH-330** (National Highway 330), which is a well-maintained 4-lane highway corridor.

Route details: Ayodhya Dham → Sultanpur Bypass → Pratapgarh Dham → Prayagraj City.

  • Average Travel Time: 3.5 to 4 hours depending on traffic exiting Pratapgarh town.
  • Road Condition: Excellent NH-330 road infrastructure with multiple food stops, fuel stations, and restrooms along the Sultanpur-Ayodhya stretch.

Direct Train Options & Timetable

For budget-conscious travelers, direct passenger and express trains run daily between the two stations. The direct train route takes about **4 to 5 hours**.

  • Saryu Express (14234): Runs daily. Leaves Ayodhya Cantt (AYC) at 02:50 AM and arrives at Prayag Junction (PRG) / Prayagraj (PRYJ) around 08:20 AM. Ideal for early morning travelers.
  • Shraddha Sethu Express (22614): Weekly service connecting the spiritual centers. Check live schedule for changes.
  • Booking Tips: Direct train tickets (especially in AC 2S / 3A coaches) should be booked via IRCTC at least 10–15 days in advance during festival seasons like Magh Mela or Kumbh Mela.

Road vs. Train: Which is Better?

Private Cabs (Recommended): Offers 100% flexibility. You can start whenever you want, stop for local Sultanpur peda sweets, and directly reach the Sangam bathing ghat. Direct drop-offs at the temples save you local auto charges in Prayagraj.

Trains: Cost-effective for solo travelers or small groups, but requires sticking to fixed timetables and managing local transport from Prayagraj station to the ghats (approx. 8 km away).
