Ayodhya Varanasi Ganga Aarti Guide: Timings, Ghats & Yatra Plan

Witnessing the evening river aarti is the most spiritually uplifting highlight of any North Indian pilgrimage. The grand Ganga Aarti in Varanasi (Kashi) and the serene Saryu Aarti in Ayodhya represent the pinnacle of Hindu devotion. In this guide, we provide the latest timings, locations, boat booking rates, and a structured itinerary to experience both aartis seamlessly.

1. Varanasi Ganga Aarti (Dashashwamedh & Assi Ghat)

Kashi's Ganga Aarti is a choreographed ritual performed by young priests draped in saffron, accompanied by bells, incense, and large brass lamps.

  • Primary Location: Dashashwamedh Ghat (Grandest scale). Assi Ghat also hosts a beautiful morning aarti (Subah-e-Banaras).
  • Timings:
    • Summer: 6:45 PM to 7:30 PM (approx)
    • Winter: 6:00 PM to 6:45 PM (approx)
  • Best View (Boat Ride Booking): While you can watch from the steps, renting a local wooden boat or booking a luxury Ganga cruise offers the best view. Normal boats cost ₹200-₹300 per seat on sharing basis, while private boats range from ₹1,500 to ₹3,500.

2. Ayodhya Saryu Aarti (Naya Ghat / Ram Ki Paidi)

Although commonly searched as "Ayodhya Ganga Aarti", the sacred river flowing through Ayodhya is the **Saryu River**. Saryu Aarti is known for its peaceful, devotional chants and beautiful reflexions on the steps of Ram Ki Paidi.

  • Primary Location: Naya Ghat (Aarti steps) and Ram Ki Paidi.
  • Timings:
    • Summer: 6:30 PM to 7:15 PM
    • Winter: 5:30 PM to 6:15 PM
  • Ghat Sightseeing: We recommend arriving 1 hour early to walk along the newly renovated Ram Ki Paidi, take holy dips, and secure a front seat near the main aarti platform.

3. Recommended 4-Day Ayodhya Varanasi Aarti & Dharshan Tour

To experience both aartis comfortably, we recommend this optimized travel itinerary. For a full breakdown of thalis, hotels, and transport costs for this circuit, check our Ayodhya yatra cost planner.

  • Day 1: Arrival in Varanasi & Evening Ganga Aarti
    Arrive in Kashi. Check into your hotel. In the evening, take a private boat ride from Dashashwamedh Ghat to experience the spectacular Ganga Aarti from the river.
  • Day 2: Kashi Vishwanath Temple & Sarnath Tour
    Early morning Ganga Snan and Kashi Vishwanath Temple darshan (including Annapurna and Vishalakshi Devi). Post lunch, visit Sarnath (Buddhist Pilgrimage Site).
  • Day 3: Varanasi to Ayodhya & Evening Saryu Aarti
    Take the morning direct train (Vande Bharat Express) or travel by private AC taxi to Ayodhya (approx 4.5 hours). Check-in and head to Saryu Ghat (Naya Ghat) for the peaceful evening Saryu Aarti.
  • Day 4: Ram Janmabhoomi Darshan & Departure
    Morning VIP Darshan at the grand Ram Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, and Kanak Bhawan. Departure from Ayodhya Junction or airport.

Tips for Senior Citizens & Families

Crucial Advice: Both aartis attract huge crowds, especially on weekends and festivals. For senior citizens, we recommend:
  1. Booking boat tickets in advance in Varanasi to avoid walking through crowded ghat steps.
  2. Arranging hotel stays within a 1-2 km radius of the ghats to avoid heavy e-rickshaw traffic congestion.
  3. Carrying small folding chairs if they cannot sit on the concrete ghat steps for 45 minutes.

Varanasi me Ganga Aarti dekhne ke liye best ghat kaunsa hai?

Dashashwamedh Ghat par sabse grand scale par aarti hoti hai. Agar aap thodi shaant aur upnishadik shaili me aarti dekhna chahte hain, toh Assi Ghat par subah 5:00 baje 'Subah-e-Banaras' program me ja sakte hain.

Boat booking ke kitne charges hote hain?

Ganga Aarti ke samay sharing wooden boats ka fare ₹200 to ₹350 per head hota hai. Private small boats ka price demand ke hisab se ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 tak jata hai.
