Varanasi Air Show 2026: Wings Over Ganges Schedule, Namo Ghat Timings & Boat Booking Guide

Published by Kashi Dharshan Local Editorial Team | Updated on 20 October 2026

⚡ Quick Event Summary: The Indian Air Force (IAF) is staging its historic mega air show titled "Wings Over Ganges" in Varanasi from 22 to 25 October 2026. Hosted at the scenic riverfront amphitheater of Namo Ghat (Khidkiya Ghat), the skies of Kashi will witness heart-pounding aerobatics by the 9-aircraft Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team (SKAT), Sarang Helicopter Display Team, and thunderous sonic flypasts by frontline supersonic fighter jets including the Dassault Rafale, Sukhoi Su-30MKI, Tejas LCA, and LCH Prachand.

1. Official Dates & Hourly Flypast Schedule

The multi-day aerial display coincides with the auspicious celebration of the IAF's 94th Anniversary and the holy spiritual festival of Sharad Purnima. Tens of thousands of pilgrims, aviation enthusiasts, and travelers from across India are converging on Varanasi's ghats.

Date Event Segment Timing Slot Key Flight Manoeuvres
22 October 2026 (Thursday) Official Flight Practice & Route Calibration 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM Fighter jet riverbank reconnaissance, Sukhoi Su-30MKI low passes, and initial formation runs over Malviya Bridge.
24 October 2026 (Saturday) Full Dress Rehearsal (Full Aerobatics) 10:30 AM – 01:00 PM Full sequence rehearsal with Surya Kiran 9-aircraft diamond loops, Sarang helicopter cross-overs, and Rafale vertical climbs.
25 October 2026 (Sunday - Sharad Purnima) Grand Mega Air Show Finale 10:30 AM – 01:30 PM Official grand demonstration featuring tricolor saffron-white-green smoke trails over Maa Ganga, sonic booms, combined Army-Navy-Air Force flypast, and high-G combat maneuvers.

2. Participating IAF Aircraft & Aerobatic Display Teams

✈️ Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team (SKAT)

Flying 9 British Aerospace Hawk Mk 132 advanced jet trainers, SKAT will paint the ancient skies of Kashi with saffron, white, and green smoke while executing barrel rolls, inverted flight, and high-speed head-on passes.

🚁 Sarang Helicopter Team

The world's premier 4-helicopter display team flying HAL Dhruv (ALH) helicopters with dazzling peacock-tail liveries, performing synchronized pirouettes and dolphins over the Ganga riverbed.

⚡ Supersonic Fighter Jets

Witness the sheer power of 4.5-generation Dassault Rafale jets with Meteor missiles capability, twin-engine Sukhoi Su-30MKI supermaneuverable fighters, and indigenous LCA Tejas cutting through the skies.

🛡️ Combat & Heavy Transports

Showcasing modern tactical airlift with the Airbus C-295, Russian heavy-lift IL-76 Gajraj, AN-32 transport aircraft, and indigenous Light Combat Helicopter (LCH) Prachand high-altitude gunship.

3. Best Viewpoints: Ghats vs. Private Boat on River Ganga

Choosing the right spot to witness the air show is critical, as over 2 to 3 lakh spectators are expected along the northern ghats:

  1. Namo Ghat (Khidkiya Ghat) — The Official Ground:
    Features the giant stainless-steel Namaste sculptures, viewing decks, open promenades, and public PA commentary systems. Note: Extremely crowded; entry will be restricted once capacity is reached.
  2. Private Boat on River Ganga (⭐ Top Recommendation):
    Watching the jets swoop down low over the holy river while seated on a private wooden rowboat, motorboat, or VIP double-decker Bajra is an unforgettable experience. You get 360-degree open sky views, zero crowd obstruction, and ideal lighting for photography. Check verified Ganga Aarti & River Boat Rental Rates to pre-book your boat before local quotas sell out.
  3. Rajghat & Adi Keshav Ghat:
    Directly adjacent to Namo Ghat with elevated stone steps providing clear sightlines toward Malviya Bridge.
  4. Rooftop Ghat Terraces & Dashashwamedh Ghat:
    While the display is centered near Namo Ghat, fighter jets performing large turning loops and sonic sweeps will be visible across the entire 84-ghat riverfront arc all the way to Assi Ghat.

  • Vehicle Diversions: Traffic police have placed vehicular restrictions on the GT Road stretch towards Rajghat, Malviya Bridge, and Chowk-Godowlia roads between 08:00 AM and 02:00 PM on show days.
  • Airport Transfers: If arriving at Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS), ensure you book a dedicated AC cab with city route clearance. See our verified Airport to Kashi Vishwanath Taxi Fare Card.
  • Arrival Time: Reach Namo Ghat or your boat boarding point at least 90 minutes prior (by 09:00 AM) to clear security checkpoints.
  • What to Carry: Sun hats, sunglasses, drinking water, photo ID cards, and telephoto DSLR cameras (professional zoom photography is permitted from spectator zones).

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. What are the dates for the Varanasi Air Show 2026?
The air show runs from 22 to 25 October 2026, with opening rehearsals on Oct 22, full-dress practice on Oct 24, and the grand finale on Oct 25 (Sharad Purnima).

Q2. Can I bring cameras to photograph the fighter jets?
Yes, cameras and telephoto lenses are allowed in public ghat viewing areas and on private boats. Drones are strictly prohibited due to defense airspace regulations.

Q3. How can I book a boat for the Air Show?
You can book directly by sending a WhatsApp message to Kashi Dharshan at +91-7408763401 or calling +91-7011960307 with your preferred date and group size.

Q4. Will regular boat services on the Ganga operate during the Air Show?
Boats will be permitted only in designated safe river corridors demarcated by the River Police and NDRF. Pre-booking ensures your boat has valid security clearance.
