What is Sugam VIP Darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Temple?

The Sugam VIP Darshan Pass is a special priority entry ticket issued by the Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust. It allows pilgrims to skip the massive general queue and enter the temple sanctum (Garbhagriha) directly through a designated VIP gate with dedicated pandits assisting the darshan.
