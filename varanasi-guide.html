Varanasi (Kashi) — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide 2026

1. Shri Kashi Vishwanath Mandir & VIP Sugam Pass

Shri Kashi Vishwanath Mandir is one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Located inside the newly constructed 5-Lakh Sq. Ft. Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor, the temple draws millions of pilgrims every year.

Devotees can enter through Gate No. 4 (Chhatta Dwar / Ganesh Gate) for priority Sugam Darshan passes, allowing fast-track entry without standing in general 4-hour queues. Sparsh Darshan is open during early morning hours (after Mangala Aarti).

2. Dashashwamedh Ghat Evening Ganga Aarti

Every evening at 6:30 PM, Dashashwamedh Ghat transforms into a grand arena of divine light. Seven priests perform synchronized Vedic rituals with heavy brass lamps, incense, and conch shells.

To get an unobstructed view, Kashi Dharshan arranges private reserved seats on hand-driven boats anchored directly in front of the ghat riverfront.

3. Kaal Bhairav & Annapurna Mandir

Known as the Kotwal (Guardian) of Kashi, visiting Kaal Bhairav Mandir is considered essential for completing a pilgrimage to Varanasi. Devotees receive sacred black threads (Kala Dhaaga) for protection.

Located right next to the Kashi Vishwanath corridor is Shri Annapurna Mandir, dedicated to the Goddess of Nourishment, where free Mahaprasad is served daily.

4. Sarnath Buddhist Circuit & Dhamek Stupa

Located just 10 km from Varanasi center, Sarnath is where Bhagwan Buddha delivered his first sermon after attaining enlightenment. Key highlights include the giant 128-foot Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, Mulagandha Kuti Vihar, and the Archaeological Museum housing the original 4-lion Ashoka Pillar capital.
