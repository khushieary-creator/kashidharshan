Varanasi Dev Deepawali 2026 Traffic Advisory: Route Diversions, Parking Lots & Boat Safety Guidelines

Published: 18 November 2026 • Author: Kashi Dharshan Logistics & Pilgrimage Team • 7 min read

🚦 Essential Traffic & Safety Summary (24 November 2026)

  • No-Vehicle Zone Timings: 12:00 PM (Noon) to 11:00 PM on Tuesday, 24 November 2026.
  • Strictly Sealed Sectors: Godowlia Crossing, Dashashwamedh Ghat Road, Maidagin to Chowk, Sonarpura to Bhelupur, Luxa, and Assi Ghat circle (Zero commercial vehicles, auto-rickshaws, or private cars).
  • Primary Parking Grounds: Beniabagh Underground Parking, Town Hall (Maidagin), Sampurnanand Sanskrit University, Machhodari Ground, and Sigra Stadium.
  • River Boat Boarding Cutoff: Tourists must board boats by 03:30 PM – 04:00 PM. Water police seals boarding points at 04:30 PM sharp.
  • Mandatory Life Jackets: 100% compliance enforced by NDRF & River Police. No life jacket = immediate boat seizure.
  • Emergency Police & Yatra Assistance: Dial 112 (Police) or call Kashi Dharshan verified local helpline at +91 70119 60307.

Why Traffic Planning is Crucial for Dev Deepawali in Kashi

On Kartik Purnima (Tuesday, 24 November 2026), an estimated 12 to 15 Lakh pilgrims and tourists from India and overseas converge upon Varanasi's 84 ghats. To ensure public safety, prevent human stampedes, and facilitate VIP movements, the Varanasi Police Commissionerate implements strict traffic diversions and converts the entire 5 km riverfront belt into a pedestrian-only zone.

Tourists arriving without prior knowledge of parking locations often get stranded in traffic jams 4–6 km away from the ghats, frequently missing their pre-booked sunset boat rides. This guide outlines the exact route maps, parking capacities, and local survival rules so your Kashi Darshan experience remains flawless.

Official Route Diversions by Origin Direction

Check where your vehicle will be stopped based on where you are entering Varanasi from:

✈️ From Babatpur Airport / Lucknow NH-31

Vehicles will be diverted at Shivpur and Bhojubir. No direct vehicles allowed towards Cantt Station after 02:00 PM. Park at Sampurnanand University Ground or take ring road to Namo Ghat plateau.

🚆 From Cantt Railway Station (Varanasi Jn)

Vehicles heading towards Godowlia will be stopped at Andhrapul and Sigra Crossing. Passengers must either walk or hire e-rickshaws up to Beniabagh, walking the remaining 800m to Dashashwamedh.

🚗 From Prayagraj / Mirzapur Highway

Vehicles entering via Akhari bypass will be stopped at Lanka / BHU Gate. Park at BHU Amphitheatre ground or Ravindrapuri, and walk 1 km to reach Assi Ghat.

NDRF & Water Police Boat Safety Rules on River Ganga

Due to the presence of over 3,000 boats navigating simultaneously during the evening diya illumination, the Varanasi District Magistrate and National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) enforce non-negotiable river safety mandates:

📋 River Ganga Dev Deepawali Safety Code:

  1. Mandatory Life Jackets: Every single boat occupant must wear a fastened life jacket from boarding until disembarking. Boat operators without sufficient life jackets are debarred from the river.
  2. Boarding Cutoff at 04:30 PM: To prevent dangerous river crowding, police barriers at all 84 ghat stairs will halt passenger boat boarding at 04:30 PM. Always arrive at your designated boarding ghat by 03:30 PM.
  3. Capacity Enforcement: Hand-rowed wooden boats (max 6-8 persons), medium motorboats (max 20-25 persons), and double-decker Bajras (strictly limited to licensed capacity). Overcrowded boats will be stopped mid-stream by water police speedboats.
  4. Zero Alcohol & Firecracker Ban: Consuming alcohol, bursting firecrackers, or carrying flammable gas on boats is strictly illegal and subject to immediate detention.
  5. Laser Show Viewing Corridor: Boats viewing the Chet Singh Ghat 3D Laser Show (07:00 PM & 08:30 PM) must drop anchor at designated deep-water buoys and maintain safe distance from heritage walls.

Pro-Tips for Families & Senior Citizens

  • Choose Namo Ghat or Rajghat for Elderly: Dashashwamedh and Assi Ghat experience intense human density. Namo Ghat offers wide ramps, easy car drop-offs, clean facilities, and direct boat boarding with less physical strain.
  • Pre-Book Vishwanath Sugam Darshan for Morning: Avoid trying to do temple darshan during peak evening hours (04:00 PM – 09:00 PM) when millions are navigating the alleys. Complete your darshan during the morning slot (06:00 AM – 11:00 AM) and keep your evening dedicated to boat viewing.
  • Keep Mobile Batteries Charged & Carry Cash: Mobile data towers near Godowlia and Dashashwamedh become heavily throttled due to extreme user density. Digital UPI payments often fail; always carry sufficient physical cash.

Frequently Asked Questions (Traffic & Safety Advisory)

Q1: What time does the no-vehicle zone start in Varanasi on Dev Deepawali?

The no-vehicle zone is enforced from 12:00 PM (Noon) to 11:00 PM on Tuesday, 24 November 2026. Key roads including Godowlia, Maidagin, Dashashwamedh, and Assi are closed to four-wheelers and autos.

Q2: Where should I park if I want to go to Dashashwamedh Ghat?

Beniabagh Underground Parking is the best authorized facility for Dashashwamedh Ghat, located approximately 1.2 km (15 minutes walk) from the riverfront.

Q3: What time should I reach the ghat to board my boat?

You must arrive by 03:30 PM to 04:00 PM. River police seals ghat boarding staircases at 04:30 PM to manage river safety.

Q4: Are life jackets compulsory for everyone on the boat?

Yes, 100% compulsory. Water police and NDRF patrol boats inspect all vessels; anyone without a life jacket is not permitted on the river.
