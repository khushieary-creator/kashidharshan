Ayodhya to Varanasi Taxi Fare: Book Cabs & Innova Crysta

If you are planning to travel between the holy cities of Lord Ram and Lord Shiva, booking a private AC cab is the most flexible, door-to-door option. The distance of 206 km is highly comfortable for families, senior citizens, and group pilgrims. Below is the complete guide to **Ayodhya to Varanasi taxi fares** for different vehicle categories.

Ayodhya to Varanasi Cab Fares Table (2026 Rates)

Vehicle Type Capacity One-Way Fare Best For
Sedan (Dzire, Etios) 4 Pax ₹3,500 - ₹3,800 Small families & budget travelers
Compact SUV (Ertiga) 6 Pax ₹4,800 - ₹5,200 Medium families with luggage
Premium SUV (Innova Crysta) 6-7 Pax ₹6,000 - ₹6,500 Maximum comfort & senior citizens
Tempo Traveller 12-17 Pax ₹11,000 - ₹13,000 Large groups & joint family yatras

*Note: Above fares are inclusive of toll taxes, state taxes, driver allowance, and GST. Fares might vary slightly during high festival rushes (like Deepotsav or Kartik Poornima).

NH 135A Highway Route and Pit Stops

The road route takes you through **Ayodhya - Akbarpur - Shahganj - Jaunpur - Varanasi**. The total driving time is approximately 4.5 hours. For dining stops, Akbarpur and Jaunpur offer clean vegetarian dhabas serving hygienic Sattvic food options without onion and garlic.

Booking Guidelines: For direct, verified transfers, always hire licensed operators. You can book an all-inclusive Ayodhya to Varanasi transit package that integrates hotel pickups and local temple darshans.
