1. Why the Ayodhya Deepotsav & Kashi Dev Deepawali Combined Yatra is Magical

Between late October and mid-November (Kartik month), Uttar Pradesh transforms into a kingdom of pure golden light. In Ayodhya Dham, the newly inaugurated Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir celebrates Deepotsav with mesmerizing laser shows, Ramlila processions, and millions of twinkling diyas at Ram Ki Paidi. Exactly 15 days later on Kartik Purnima, Varanasi hosts Dev Deepawali, where every single ghat from Assi to Rajghat glows with oil lamps, accompanied by Vedic Ganga Aarti and fireworks across the Ganges.

2. Day-by-Day Festival Tour Itinerary (5 Days / 4 Nights)

  • Day 1 (Arrival & Ayodhya Deepotsav Spectacle): Pickup from Ayodhya Dham Airport (AYJ) or Lucknow. Check in to premium hotel. Attend afternoon VIP Darshan at Ram Mandir and Hanuman Garhi. By 05:00 PM, take reserved viewing seats at Ram Ki Paidi to witness the lighting of 25+ lakh diyas and the mega Saryu aerial drone show. Overnight stay in Ayodhya.
  • Day 2 (Ayodhya Sightseeing to Prayagraj Sangam): Morning holy bath at Saryu Ghat. Visit Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal. Drive to Prayagraj (160 km, 3.5 hrs). Take a private wooden boat for Triveni Sangam Snan and visit the miraculous Lete Hanuman Mandir. Overnight stay in Prayagraj.
  • Day 3 (Prayagraj to Kashi Vishwanath Dham): Scenic drive from Prayagraj to Varanasi (125 km, 2.5 hrs via NH-19). Hotel check-in near Godowlia Corridor. Evening heritage ghat walk and preliminary Ganga Aarti viewing at Dashashwamedh Ghat.
  • Day 4 (Kashi Vishwanath VIP Darshan & Dev Deepawali Boat Ride): Morning Sugam Darshan at Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple, followed by visits to Kal Bhairav and Sarnath Buddhist Circuit. At 04:30 PM, board your pre-reserved private Bajra or Motorboat to witness the world-famous 84 Ghats Dev Deepawali illumination, Chet Singh Ghat 3D Projection Mapping show, and grand Ganga Maha Aarti.
  • Day 5 (Subah-e-Banaras & Departure): Sunrise boat ride from Assi Ghat for Subah-e-Banaras Vedic chanting. Enjoy hot Banarasi Kachori-Jalebi breakfast. Shopping in Vishwanath Gali for Banarasi sarees and drop at Varanasi Airport (VNS) or Railway Station (BSB).

3. How to Secure Private Boat Booking for Dev Deepawali

Boat booking on Dev Deepawali is notorious for extreme price inflation and last-minute cancellations by unregulated boatmen. Here are essential tips:

  • Reserve 30–45 Days in Advance: Registered boats in Varanasi have a fixed passenger quota strictly enforced by the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) and Water Police. Last-minute boat rentals often exceed ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 for simple motorboats.
  • Choose the Right Boat Category: For extended family groups, a double-decker Bajra with sofa seating and upper observation deck offers the best photographic views. For couples and small families, private motorboats provide rapid movement between Assi and Manikarnika.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Can we perform Pinda Daan or Ganga Snan during Dev Deepawali?

Yes, taking a holy dip on Kartik Purnima (Dev Deepawali) in the sacred Ganges is considered equivalent to a thousand Ashwamedha Yajnas. Special ritual spaces are arranged at Dashashwamedh and Panchganga Ghat.

Q: Are vehicles permitted near Ghats on Dev Deepawali?

No, vehicular traffic is strictly prohibited within 3 km of the riverfront (Godowlia, Maidagin, Sonarpura) from 02:00 PM to midnight. Our packages include hotels situated within comfortable walking distance of the Corridor.
