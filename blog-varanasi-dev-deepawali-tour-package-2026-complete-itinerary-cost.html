Dev Deepawali (the "Diwali of the Gods") is celebrated exactly 15 days after Diwali on Kartik Purnima. On this divine night, it is believed that the 33 crore Hindu deities descend to the holy city of Kashi to take a dip in River Ganga and celebrate Lord Shiva's victory over the demon Tripurasura.

2. 3-Day Dev Deepawali Master Itinerary (23 to 25 November 2026)

Day 1 (23 Nov): Arrival in Kashi, Temple Visits & Pre-Festive Vibrations

Arrive at Varanasi Airport/Railway Station. Private AC transfer to your hotel. Visit the grand Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor using your pre-arranged VIP Sugam Darshan Pass. Visit Maa Annapurna Mandir and Kaal Bhairav. Evening walk across Godowlia and Dashashwamedh Ghat to experience the lighting preparations. Overnight stay in Varanasi.

Day 2 (24 Nov - Dev Deepawali): The Grand Divine Illumination of 84 Ghats

Early morning visit to historic Sarnath. Post lunch, relax before the mega evening. By 04:30 PM, board your reserved private or shared boat at the ghat. As the sun sets, witness all 84 ghats lit with 10 Lakh+ clay lamps (*diyas*). Watch the world-renowned 21-priest Maha Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat, followed by the spectacular 3D Laser & Sound projection show on the historic stone facade of Chet Singh Fort, concluded with sky-illuminating green fireworks across the riverbank. Return to hotel by 09:30 PM.

Day 3 (25 Nov): Subah-e-Banaras, Holy Dip & Departure

Experience serene Subah-e-Banaras sunrise Aarti at Assi Ghat. Take a holy snan in Maa Ganga, visit Sankat Mochan Temple and BHU New Vishwanath Temple. Enjoy traditional Banarasi breakfast (Kachori Jalebi & winter Malaiyo). On-time transfer to Varanasi Airport/Railway Station with blessed prasad.

3. Frequently Asked Questions (Dev Deepawali FAQs)

Can senior citizens comfortably watch Dev Deepawali?
Yes! The best and most comfortable way for senior citizens is from a pre-booked private motorboat or large Bajra. It completely avoids the massive 10-lakh pedestrian crowds on the ghat steps and allows peaceful viewing from cushioned seats.
Are cars and cabs allowed near Godowlia on Dev Deepawali?
No, the Varanasi police enforces a complete vehicle curfew (no four-wheelers) within 2 km of the ghats from 12:00 PM onwards on 24 November. Staying at a hotel walking distance to the ghats or having our local guide escort you via approved e-rickshaw routes is essential.
What is the cancellation policy for festival bookings?
Because boat slots and festival hotel rooms are strictly limited, full details are shared upon quotation. Free date adjustments or partial refunds are provided as per our transparent pilgrimage terms.
