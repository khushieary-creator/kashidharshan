Why Watch Ganga Aarti from a Boat in Varanasi?

The evening **Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat** is one of the most mesmerizing spiritual spectacles in the world. Thousands of devotees assemble at the ghat stairs every single evening. Viewing the Aarti from the calm waters of Mother Ganga on a private boat offers an unobstructed, 360-degree panoramic view of the Vedic rituals, glowing brass lamps, and chanting priests without squeezing through crowds.

Morning Subah-e-Banaras Sunrise Boat Ride

Besides the evening Aarti, the Subah-e-Banaras sunrise boat ride (5:30 AM to 7:30 AM) is an absolute must-do. You sail from Assi Ghat to Manikarnika Ghat & Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor while watching the morning sun illuminate the ancient pink sandstone palaces along the riverbank.

Best Ghats Included in the 84-Ghat Boat Tour

  • 🚩 Dashashwamedh Ghat: The main hub of grand evening Aarti.
  • 🛕 Kashi Vishwanath Corridor Ghat: Direct grand river entrance to Lord Shiva's abode.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. What time should I board the boat for evening Ganga Aarti?

Boats should be boarded by 5:15 PM in winter and 6:00 PM in summer to get the best front-row position facing the Aarti stage.

Q2. Are life jackets provided on boats in Varanasi?

Yes, all registered boats arranged by Kashi Dharshan comply with UP Tourism safety norms and provide certified life jackets for every passenger.
