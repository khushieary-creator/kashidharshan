What is Sugam Darshan (VIP Entry)?

The temple trust manages two types of entry tracks: General Entry and **Sugam Darshan (VIP Entry)**. The Sugam Darshan passes are digital barcode tickets that grant you access to a dedicated shorter lane. This VIP lane bypasses the primary general queue barricades, lowering your overall wait time inside the temple corridor to under 15-20 minutes. It is highly recommended for families traveling with children or elderly pilgrims.

Official VIP Pass Price List & Rules

There is a lot of misinformation online regarding the cost of passes. Please read the official details below carefully:

  • Official Trust Fee: **₹0 (Free of Cost)**. The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust does not charge any ticket fee for VIP or Sugam Darshan passes.
  • Authorized Source: Passes can ONLY be reserved through the official trust website using your verified mobile number and a government-approved identity card (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, etc.).

Step-by-Step Booking Method

If you want to try booking the passes online yourself, follow these steps:

  1. Visit the official Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra trust portal.
  2. Login using your active mobile number and enter the received OTP.
  3. Go to the **"Darshan"** or **"Aarti"** section.
  4. Select your preferred date, timings slot, and enter the details of all devotees (Name, Age, Gender, ID type & ID number).
  5. Upload a clear scanned copy of your ID.
  6. Once successful, download the digital pass. You must carry the same physical ID card used in the booking when visiting the temple gate.

Note: Online slots are opened 15 days in advance and usually book out within minutes.
