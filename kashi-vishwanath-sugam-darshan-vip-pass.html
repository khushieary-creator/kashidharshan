1. What is Sugam Darshan at Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple?

Sugam Darshan is the official VIP pilgrimage facility introduced by the Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust. Designed especially for senior citizens, families with infants, outstation yatris on strict flight schedules, and devotees who cannot stand for hours in crowded lines, Sugam Darshan provides an air-conditioned, dedicated queue corridor that takes you directly to the silver threshold of the Garbhagriha.

3. Entry Gates & Reporting Protocol

Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham has 4 primary gates, but Sugam Darshan pilgrims must report to specific counters:

  • Gate No. 2 (Saraswati Gate - Bansphatak / Godowlia): Recommended if arriving by cab from Varanasi Cantt Station or Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport. E-rickshaws drop you directly near Bansphatak crossing.
  • Gate No. 4 (Ganga Riverfront Corridor Gate - Lalita Ghat): Recommended if you arrive via private morning boat from Dashashwamedh or Assi Ghat. You take the grand escalators or ramp directly into the temple plaza.

4. Dress Code & Prohibited Items Checklist

Recommended Attire

Traditional Indian clothing is strongly recommended. Men: Kurta-Pyjama or Dhoti. Women: Saree or Salwar Suit with Dupatta. Modest attire ensures respectful and peaceful temple entry.

Strictly Banned Inside

Mobile phones, smartwatches, cameras, Bluetooth earphones, leather belts, shoes, metal keychains, and cigarettes/tobacco. Deposit all valuables in the VIP lockers at Gate 2.
