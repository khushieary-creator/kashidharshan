Delhi to Ayodhya 3-Day Yatra Itinerary

Designed specifically for travelers arriving from New Delhi / NCR via flight or express trains.

Day 1 — Ayodhya Arrival & Saryu Aarti

Departure from Delhi & Hotel Check-in

Depart from Delhi via morning flight (DEL to AYJ) or Vande Bharat Express. On arrival, our private AC cab driver will pick you up and transfer you to the hotel. In the evening, attend the divine Saryu Aarti and visit Ram Ki Paidi.

Day 2 — Ram Janmabhoomi VIP Darshan

Hanuman Garhi & Ram Mandir

Start your day with morning prayers at Hanuman Garhi. Proceed to the grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir with our pre-booked priority VIP Darshan passes (reducing public wait times). Afternoon sightseeing covers Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, and Mani Parvat.

Day 3 — Local Sightseeing & Return to Delhi

Farewell & Departure

Visit Nageshwarnath Temple and Guptar Ghat (where Lord Ram took Jal Samadhi). In the afternoon, check out from the hotel. The private cab will drop you at Ayodhya Airport or Railway Station for your return flight/train to Delhi.
