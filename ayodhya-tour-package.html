Frequently Asked Questions — Ayodhya Tour Package 2026

How to book Ram Mandir VIP Sugam Darshan pass in Ayodhya?+
Ram Mandir Sugam Darshan passes can be arranged through the official trust portal or via our registered Ayodhya yatra coordinator. Our packages include priority darshan assistance with direct entry, allowing devotees to complete darshan comfortably within 20 to 30 minutes without waiting in 3-hour general queues.
What is the cost of an Ayodhya tour package in 2026?+
Ayodhya tour packages start at ₹2,900 per vehicle for a Same-Day private AC cab tour (covering Ram Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, and Saryu Aarti). A 2-Day/1-Night package with 3-star hotel stay, breakfast, and VIP darshan escort starts at ₹5,500 per person.
How to reach Ayodhya by flight or train?+
Ayodhya is directly connected by air via Maharishi Valmiki International Airport (AYJ) with non-stop flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Ahmedabad, and Kolkata. By rail, Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY) and Ayodhya Cantt (AYC) receive trains from all major Indian cities. We provide doorstep cab pickup from all airports and stations.
Which sacred temples are included in the Ayodhya tour package?+
Our Ayodhya tour packages cover Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Hanuman Garhi Mandir, Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal, Saryu River Ghats (Ram Ki Paidi), Guptar Ghat, Surya Kund, and Bharat Kund.
Is wheelchair or golf-cart assistance available for senior citizens in Ayodhya?+
Yes, we provide dedicated wheelchair escorts and electric golf-cart assistance along Ram Path and temple security corridors for elderly parents and differently-abled pilgrims.
