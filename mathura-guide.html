Mathura Dham — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide

Complete spiritual & travel guide to Mathura Dham. Learn about Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi, Dwarkadhish Mandir, Vishram Ghat & Yamuna Aarti.

Mathura Dham, the divine birthplace of Lord Shri Krishna, is the spiritual capital of Braj Bhoomi situated along the sacred River Yamuna.

From Garbha Griha at Janmabhoomi to evening Yamuna Aarti at Vishram Ghat, Mathura offers immense devotion and rich heritage.

1. Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi (Garbha Griha)

The exact prison cell (Garbha Griha) where Lord Krishna was born to Devaki and Vasudeva. Features the grand Keshavdeva Temple.

2. Dwarkadhish Mandir

Built in 1814, famous for its grand Rajasthani architecture, Jhula festival during monsoon, and beautiful deity of Lord Krishna as King of Dwarka.

3. Vishram Ghat & Evening Yamuna Aarti

The central ghat where Lord Krishna rested after killing Kansa. Every evening, brass lamps and chants illuminate the holy Yamuna waters.

4. Gita Mandir & Birla Temple

Features the entire 18 chapters of Bhagavad Gita carved into marble pillars surrounded by lush serene gardens.
