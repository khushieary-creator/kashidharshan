1-Day Varanasi Local Sightseeing Itinerary

Varanasi (Banaras) is a city of spiritual wonders. Covering the main temples and historical sites in a single day is best done with a private AC cab. Below is the ideal 1-day itinerary recommended by Kashi Dharshan:

  • 🌅 6:00 AM – 8:30 AM: Subah-e-Banaras Sunrise Boat Ride at Assi Ghat & Holy Ganga Dip.
  • 🛕 9:00 AM – 11:30 AM: Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor, Annapurna Mandir & Kaal Bhairav (Kotwal of Kashi).
  • 🛕 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM: Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, Durga Kund & New Vishwanath Temple inside BHU Campus.
  • 🍱 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM: Traditional Sattvik Banarasi Lunch & Lassi break.
  • 🏛️ 3:30 PM – 5:30 PM: Sarnath Tour — Dhamek Stupa, Ashoka Pillar & Archaeological Museum.
  • ⛵ 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM: Grand Evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat from boat.

Vehicles drop passengers at Godowlia Crossing (300 meters from Gate No. 4) due to pedestrian corridor zones. E-rickshaws and golf carts operate from Godowlia.
