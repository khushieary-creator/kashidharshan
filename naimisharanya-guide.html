Naimisharanya Dham — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide 2026

Puranic Yatra Guide4 min readPublished 2026
In this guide
  1. Chakra Tirth Holy Kunda
  2. Maa Lalita Devi Shakti Peeth
  3. Vyas Gaddi & Soot Gaddi
  4. Hanuman Garhi (Dakshinamukhi)

1. Chakra Tirth Holy Kunda

The circular water body created by Lord Vishnu's Sudarshana Chakra. A dip in Chakra Tirth cleanses all karmic sins.

2. Maa Lalita Devi Shakti Peeth

One of the prominent 51 Shakti Peethas where the heart of Goddess Sati fell. Devotees seek blessings for family health and prosperity.

3. Vyas Gaddi & Soot Gaddi

The sacred Banyan tree underneath which Sage Ved Vyas divided the Vedas and composed the 18 Puranas.

4. Hanuman Garhi (Dakshinamukhi)

Home to a unique South-facing Lord Hanuman idol carrying Ram & Laxman on his shoulders after defeating Ahiravana.
