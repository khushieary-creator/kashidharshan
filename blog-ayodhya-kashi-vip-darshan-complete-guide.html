What is Sugam Darshan (VIP Entry)?

The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust provides dedicated priority queue passes known as Sugam Darshan. Here is how it works:

  • Dedicated Priority Gate: Pass holders enter through a separate, accelerated queue line (Gate No. 11 / Sugam Darshan Path).
  • Reduced Wait Time: Average entry-to-darshan time drops from 120 minutes down to just **15–20 minutes**.
  • Official Fee: Official Sugam Darshan passes issued by the Trust are **100% FREE**.

5 Insider Hacks to Secure Slots Online

Because thousands of pilgrims attempt ramlala vip darshan online daily, slots sell out fast. Follow these 5 proven hacks:

  1. Know the Refresh Time: Slot releases on the official trust portal typically refresh at 00:00 AM midnight (15 days prior). Be logged in with your mobile number 5 minutes prior.
  2. Keep Government IDs Ready: Have clear JPG/PNG scans of your Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID for every family member (max 4 members per booking token).
  3. Target Afternoon Slots: Morning 07:00 AM slots get snapped up first. Opting for **02:30 PM to 04:00 PM** slots increases your booking success rate significantly.
  4. Carry Printed Hardcopies: Digital PDFs on mobile phones are often delayed at security checkpoints. Always carry printed passes along with original physical ID cards.
  5. Pre-Book Hotel Stays Near Ram Path: Staying at hotel corridors close to Hanuman Garhi saves you 45 minutes of local auto traffic jams.

Kashi Vishwanath VIP Entry Rules (Varanasi)

Combining Ayodhya with Kashi (Varanasi) is the most popular pilgrim circuit. At Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham:

  • Sugam Darshan Ticket Price: ₹300 per person (issued officially by KV Temple Trust).
  • Sparsh Darshan: Allowed during early morning hours depending on crowd regulations.
  • Dress Code: Traditional Indian attire (Dhoti/Kurta for men, Saree/Salwar for women) is mandatory for inner sanctum entry during special pujas.

Beware of Fake Booking Touts & Unofficial Websites

Due to intense demand, several unverified third-party websites charge ₹500 to ₹1,500 claiming to sell "Ram Mandir VIP Tickets". The Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Trust does NOT sell passes through private websites. Always book directly on official trust portals or choose a registered tour operator who handles complete local logistics.
