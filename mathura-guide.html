Mathura Dham — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide 2026

Braj Yatra Guide4 min readPublished 2026
In this guide
  1. Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi (Garbha Griha)
  2. Dwarkadhish Mandir
  3. Vishram Ghat & Evening Yamuna Aarti
  4. Gita Mandir & Birla Temple

1. Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi (Garbha Griha)

The exact prison cell (Garbha Griha) where Lord Krishna was born to Devaki and Vasudeva. Features the grand Keshavdeva Temple.

2. Dwarkadhish Mandir

Built in 1814, famous for its grand Rajasthani architecture, Jhula festival during monsoon, and beautiful deity of Lord Krishna.

3. Vishram Ghat & Evening Yamuna Aarti

The central ghat where Lord Krishna rested after killing Kansa. Every evening, brass lamps and chants illuminate holy Yamuna.

4. Gita Mandir & Birla Temple

Features the entire 18 chapters of Bhagavad Gita carved into marble pillars surrounded by lush serene gardens.
