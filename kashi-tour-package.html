Detailed 3-Day Kashi Tour Package Itinerary

Day 1 — Arrival, Hotel Check-in & Grand Evening Ganga Aarti

Welcome to Mokshapuri Kashi

Upon arrival at Varanasi Airport (VNS) or Varanasi Junction (BSB/DDU), our tour representative receives you with traditional garland welcome. Transfer in your private AC cab to your verified 3-star hotel near Godowlia/Ghats. At 05:00 PM, head to the riverfront and board a private wooden boat to witness the divine 7-priest evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat from the serene river vantage. Overnight stay in Varanasi.

Day 2 — Kashi Vishwanath VIP Darshan, Kaal Bhairav & Sarnath

Sacred Temple Trail & Buddha's Land

Early morning breakfast, followed by priority VIP entry through Gate 4 Chhatta Dwar using your pre-booked Kashi Vishwanath Sugam Darshan Pass. Visit Maa Annapurna Mandir and Kaal Bhairav Temple (Kotwal of Varanasi). Post lunch, drive 10 km to historic Sarnath where Lord Buddha preached his first sermon: visit Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, and the Sarnath Archeological Museum. Evening free for Banarasi silk saree shopping. Overnight stay in Varanasi.

Day 3 — Subah-e-Banaras Sunrise Boat, BHU & Departure

Dawn Meditation & Sacred Farewell

Wake up at 05:15 AM to experience the world-famous Subah-e-Banaras at Assi Ghat with Vedic hawan, classical morning raga, and a calm sunrise boat tour to Manikarnika Ghat. Visit Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, Durga Kund, and New Vishwanath Temple (Birla Temple) at Banaras Hindu University. Return to hotel for checkout, followed by on-time drop at Varanasi Airport/Station with blessed Prasad.
