Ayodhya Dham — Complete Spiritual & Sacred Sightseeing Guide

Complete spiritual & travel guide to Ayodhya Dham. Learn about Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Saryu Aarti & Ghats.

Ayodhya Dham, the sacred birthplace of Lord Shri Ram on the holy banks of River Saryu, is the eternal center of faith and devotion.

1. Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir

The majestic grand temple of Ram Lalla. Enter through Sugam Darshan passes or general queues. Morning & evening Aarti bookings provide uninterrupted darshan experience.

2. Hanuman Garhi Mandir

Traditionally, pilgrims visit Hanuman Garhi first to seek the blessings of Lord Hanuman, the protective guardian deity of Ayodhya Dham.

3. Kanak Bhawan & Dashrath Mahal

Kanak Bhawan, gifted to Mata Sita by Queen Kaikeyi, is famous for its divine idols of Lord Ram and Mata Sita adorned with golden crowns.

4. Saryu Ghat & Evening Deepotsav Aarti

Ram ki Paidi and Naya Ghat sparkle with thousands of oil lamps during the evening Saryu Aarti, offering a tranquil and uplifting atmosphere.
