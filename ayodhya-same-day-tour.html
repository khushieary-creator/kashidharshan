1-Day Spiritual Journey

Ayodhya Same Day Tour Package

Explore the sacred sites of Lord Ram's birthplace within a single day. Perfect for families, senior citizens, and travelers with limited time. VIP entry support, private AC cabs, and local pandit guides included.

The Perfect 1-Day Itinerary

This tour is meticulously planned to cover all main spiritual landmarks comfortably. Below is the scheduled layout for your yatra:

08:30 AM — Pickup & Welcome

Morning Pickup

Our driver picks you up in a private AC vehicle from Ayodhya airport, railway station, or your hotel (also available with Lucknow transfer packages).

09:00 AM — Hanuman Garhi Temple

Blessings from Lord Hanuman

We begin the yatra at the famous hilltop temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman. Climb the 76 steps to seek safety and permission from Hanumanji to explore Ram's city.

10:30 AM — Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir

Darshan of Ram Lalla

Visit the grand, newly built Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple. Seek blessings, witness the magnificent architecture, and enjoy unhurried prayer time with our guide assisting you.

01:30 PM — Satvik Lunch Break

Traditional Veg Lunch

Enjoy a delicious, pure vegetarian Satvik lunch at a clean, handpicked local restaurant (included in Standard and Premium tiers).

03:00 PM — Kanak Bhawan & Dashrath Mahal

Explore Golden Palace

Visit the beautiful Kanak Bhawan palace gifted to Mata Sita by Queen Kaikeyi, followed by a walk through Dashrath Mahal to see the heritage and history of the Ramayana.

05:30 PM — Saryu River & Evening Aarti

Divine Sunset Aarti

Walk down to the holy Saryu River banks at Ram ki Paidi. Witness the spectacular, lamp-lit evening Saryu Aarti creating a memorable, divine atmosphere.

07:30 PM — Return Departure

Safe Drop-off

Drop-off back to the Ayodhya airport/station or Lucknow to wrap up your soulful same-day pilgrimage.

Senior Citizen & Wheelchair Care Services

We specialize in making pilgrimages comfortable for elderly parents and disabled devotees. Our dedicated care services ensure peace of mind:

  • Wheelchair Assistance: Pre-arranged wheelchair escorts at Ram Mandir and Kashi Vishwanath.

  • Dedicated Yatra Guide: Patient, local guides who manage queue entries and security checks comfortably.
  • Comfort Stays & Cars: Hotels with lift access, ground floor rooms, and spacious AC SUVs (Ertiga/Innova).

Good to know

FAQ — Same Day Yatra

Can this tour start from Lucknow or Varanasi?+
Yes! We regularly organize pickups from Lucknow (approx. 2.5 hours via Purvanchal Expressway) and Varanasi (approx. 4.5 hours by road) early in the morning and return drop-offs by night. Select the starting location in the form.
Are entry fees and VIP passes included in the price?+
Standard and Premium tier packages include entry facilitation, priority passes, and guided assistance inside the Ram Mandir. Any government entry tickets/VIP passes will be clearly quoted upfront.
What should we wear for the yatra?+
Traditional, decent Indian attire is recommended (saree/salwar suit for women, dhoti/kurta/formal trousers for men). Avoid wearing shorts or overly modern clothing inside the temples.
