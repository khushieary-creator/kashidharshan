2. How to Book Kashi Vishwanath VIP Pass Step-by-Step

  1. Select Date & Time Slot: Sugam Darshan slots are open from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM daily.
  2. Provide Visitor Details: Submit full names, age, gender, and Aadhaar card numbers for all family members.
  3. Receive QR Code PDF: Download your official barcode pass to show at Gate 4 security check points.

3. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. Which gate is best for VIP Sugam Darshan?
Gate No. 4 (Chhatta Dwar) near Godowlia Chowk is the primary VIP corridor gate offering seamless entry to the Vishwanath Corridor.

Q2. Is dress code required for Kashi Vishwanath VIP Darshan?
Yes, traditional Indian attire is recommended (Kurta-Pyjama or Dhoti for men; Saree or Salwar suit for women).
