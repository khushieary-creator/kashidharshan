Planning a pilgrimage to Ayodhya is a deeply spiritual experience. However, before setting off, many devotees ask: Ayodhya jane ka kharcha kitna hoga? (How much will an Ayodhya trip cost?). Knowing the budget requirements helps you choose the right accommodation and transit methods. Below, we break down the costs into Budget, Standard, and Luxury tiers for a 3-Day / 2-Night yatra.

1. Accommodation Costs in Ayodhya (Stays)

Since the consecration of the grand Ram Mandir, hospitality options in Ayodhya have grown significantly. The cost depends on the proximity to Ram Janmabhoomi and Hanuman Garhi:

  • Budget (Dharamshalas & Guest Houses): ₹800 to ₹1,500 per night. These offer clean basic amenities but may be a bit farther from the temple.
  • Standard (3-Star Hotels / Homestays): ₹2,500 to ₹4,500 per night. Highly recommended for families, offering clean AC rooms and pure veg dining options.
  • Luxury (4-Star / Heritage Hotels): ₹7,000 to ₹12,000+ per night. These provide premium facilities, closer proximity to temples, and custom support for senior citizens.

2. Local and Interstate Transit Charges (Travel)

The total travel cost depends on your origin city (like Delhi, Mumbai, or Kolkata). If you are flying into Lucknow Airport, check our complete Lucknow to Ayodhya taxi fare and travel guide. Once you arrive, here are the local transit costs:

  • E-Rickshaws & Autos: ₹20 to ₹50 per passenger for short commutes along Ram Path and Bhakti Path. (Auto booking for a whole day costs ₹800 to ₹1,200).
  • Local AC Taxi Cab (Full Day): ₹2,000 to ₹2,500 for a hatchback/sedan, and ₹3,500+ for SUVs (like Ertiga or Innova) for sightseeing and transfers.

3. Meals & Food Expenses

Food in Ayodhya is 100% vegetarian and highly affordable. A standard Sattvic thali without onion and garlic costs around ₹120 to ₹200 at local bhojnalayas near Hanuman Garhi. Read our dedicated Ayodhya Sattvic food guide for top recommended eateries. Dining at premium air-conditioned restaurants costs ₹300 to ₹500 per person per meal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Ayodhya ghumne ke liye minimum kitna budget chahiye?

Agar aap standard 3-star hotels aur private transport ke sath travel karna chahte hain, toh 2 logo ke liye 3-day trip ka minimum kharcha ₹17,000 se ₹20,000 ke beech aata hai. Dharamshala aur local autos ke sath aap ise ₹6,000 me bhi complete kar sakte hain.

Ram Mandir darshan pass ka ticket kitne ka aata hai?

Ram Mandir general darshan bilkul free hai. Sugam Darshan (priority pass) aur Shringar Aarti/Sandhya Aarti tickets official website se pre-book karni hoti hain (learn more in our VIP Darshan guide). Humare standard packages me hum ye passes pre-arrange kar ke dete hain.

Ayodhya tour package me kya-kya cost include hoti hai?

Humare standard tour packages me 3★/4★ hotel stays, pickup & drop transfers, private AC vehicle, expert local guide, aur darshan coordination included hote hain, jisme koi hidden charges nahi hote.
