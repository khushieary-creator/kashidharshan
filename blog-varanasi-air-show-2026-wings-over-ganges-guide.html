1. Official Dates & Hourly Flypast Schedule

The multi-day aerial display coincides with the auspicious celebration of the IAF's 94th Anniversary and the holy spiritual festival of Sharad Purnima. Tens of thousands of pilgrims, aviation enthusiasts, and travelers from across India are converging on Varanasi's riverfront to witness Indian defense prowess over Maa Ganga.

Date Event Segment Timing Slot Key Flight Manoeuvres
22 October 2026 (Thursday) Official Flight Practice & Route Calibration 10:00 AM – 12:30 PM Fighter jet riverbank reconnaissance, Sukhoi Su-30MKI low passes, and initial formation runs over Malviya Bridge.

2. Participating IAF Aircraft & Aerobatic Display Teams

✈️ Surya Kiran Aerobatic Team (SKAT)

Flying 9 British Aerospace Hawk Mk 132 advanced jet trainers, SKAT paints the ancient skies of Kashi with saffron, white, and green smoke while executing synchronized barrel rolls, inverted flight, and high-speed head-on passes.

🚁 Sarang Helicopter Team

The world's premier 4-helicopter display team flying HAL Dhruv (ALH) helicopters with dazzling peacock-tail liveries, performing synchronized pirouettes, crosses, and dolphins right above the Ganges riverbed.

⚡ Supersonic Fighter Jets

Witness the sheer power of 4.5-generation Dassault Rafale jets, twin-engine Sukhoi Su-30MKI supermaneuverable air superiority fighters, and indigenous LCA Tejas carving supersonic paths across the skyline.

🛡️ Combat & Heavy Transports

Showcasing modern tactical airlift with the Airbus C-295, Russian heavy-lift IL-76 Gajraj, AN-32 transport aircraft, and indigenous Light Combat Helicopter (LCH) Prachand high-altitude gunship.

3. Best Viewpoints Along River Ganga (100% Free Public Viewing)

To experience the best vantage points along the Varanasi riverfront, consider these prime spectator areas (all locations offer 100% free open public viewing):

  1. Namo Ghat (Khidkiya Ghat) — Central Stage:
    Features the giant stainless-steel Namaste sculptures, wide paved plazas, viewing galleries, and live public address commentary systems. Entry for general citizens is 100% free; arrive by 09:00 AM for comfortable front-row steps.
  2. Rajghat & Adi Keshav Ghat:
    Directly adjacent to Namo Ghat with elevated stone ghat steps providing clear, wide-angle sightlines towards Malviya Bridge as jets dive and climb over the water.
  3. Malviya Bridge (Rajghat Bridge) Approaches:
    Elevated vantage points ideal for aviation photographers capturing low sonic flypasts and aerobatic crosses right above the sacred waters.
  4. Dashashwamedh Ghat to Assi Ghat Arc:
    While the epicenter is Namo Ghat, fighter jets performing large tactical loops, high-altitude turns, and saffron-white-green smoke trails will be visible across the entire 84-ghat crescent of Kashi.

4. Spectator Guidelines & Free Entry Advisory

🎟️ 100% Free Entry (निःशुल्क)

Show sabhi nagrikon aur paryatakon ke liye poori tarah free hai. Kisi bhi ghat par khade hokar dekhne ke liye koi ticket ya booking charge nahi lagta.

⏰ Subah 09:00 AM tak Pahunchein

Show 10:30 AM par shuru hota hai. Bheed aur traffic diversions se bachne ke liye Namo Ghat ya Rajghat par 09:00 AM tak pahunchna aasan aur suvidhajanak rehta hai.

🚫 Drones Sakht Mana Hain

Suraksha aur defense airspace niyamavali ke tahat private drone camera udana poori tarah pratibandhit hai. Normal mobile cameras aur DSLR cameras permitted hain.

💧 Paani aur Dhoop se Bachav

Dopahar ki dhoop se bachav ke liye cap/chashma aur peene ke paani ki bottle saath rakhein. Ghat ki seedhiyon par aaram se chalne ke liye flat shoes pehnein.

5. Important Travel Advisory & Traffic Restrictions

Varanasi Traffic Police and District Administration will enforce specific movement protocols on show days:

  • Vehicle Diversions: Vehicular traffic towards Rajghat, Malviya Bridge, and Chowk-Godowlia roads will be restricted between 08:00 AM and 02:00 PM on 22, 24, and 25 October.
  • Airport Transfers: If arriving at Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (VNS), ensure you book a dedicated AC cab with city route clearance. See our verified Airport to Kashi Vishwanath Taxi Fare Card.
  • Arrival Time: Reach Namo Ghat or your viewing point at least 90 minutes prior (by 09:00 AM) to clear security checkpoints smoothly.
  • What to Carry: Sun hats, sunglasses, drinking water, photo ID cards, and telephoto DSLR cameras (telephoto zoom photography from spectator zones is permitted).

7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. What are the dates for the Varanasi Air Show 2026?

The air show runs from 22 to 25 October 2026, with opening rehearsals on Oct 22, full-dress practice on Oct 24, and the grand finale on Oct 25 (Sharad Purnima).

Q2. Can I bring cameras to photograph the fighter jets?

Yes, mobile cameras and telephoto DSLR lenses are allowed in public ghat viewing areas. Drones are strictly prohibited due to defense airspace regulations.

Q3. Is there any entry fee or ticket for the Varanasi Air Show?

No, the Varanasi Air Show is 100% FREE. Indian Air Force aur Varanasi prashasan dwara aayojit yeh show sabhi ke liye open aur bilkul muft hai. Kisi bhi ghat par dekhne ke liye koi ticket ya charge nahi lagta.

Q4. Where can I find official air show updates and Kashi yatra support?

Official event alerts and airspace advisories are announced by the Indian Air Force and Varanasi District Administration. For temple tour packages and VIP darshan passes, yatris can visit our Yatra Contact Page.
