Introduction to Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham

Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple in Varanasi (Banaras) is one of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Millions of devotees visit the holy city of Kashi every year to seek blessings, perform Sparsh Darshan, and witness the divine Ganga Aarti. Following the grand inauguration of the Kashi Vishwanath Dham Corridor connecting the ghats of the Ganga directly to the temple, pilgrim footfalls have crossed over 70 million annually.

Due to huge crowds, general queues can take anywhere between 3 to 5 hours. To ensure a smooth, comfortable experience for families, elderly pilgrims, and international travelers, the temple trust offers Sugam VIP Darshan passes and special Aarti slots.

Dress Code Rules & Prohibited Items

  • Dress Code: Traditional Indian attire is highly recommended. Men: Dhoti-Kurta or Pyjama-Kurta. Women: Saree, Salwar Kameez, or suit. Western attire like shorts, sleeveless tops, and ripped jeans are strictly discouraged inside the main sanctum.
  • Prohibited Electronics: Mobile phones, smartwatches, cameras, leather belts, wallets, and bags are not allowed inside the temple complex. Free locker facilities are available at Godowlia Gate No. 4 & Corridor entries.
