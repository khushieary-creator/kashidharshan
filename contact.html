Booking FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a yatra and pay the advance?+
You can book by sending an enquiry via our form, calling, or WhatsApping us. We require a 20% booking advance to confirm your hotels and private AC cab, with the remaining balance payable upon arrival.
What is the refund and cancellation policy?+
We offer a 100% refund on cancellations made at least 7 days before your travel date. Cancellations within 7 days may incur nominal hotel retention charges, depending on seasonal demand.
Is wheelchair support available for elderly travelers?+
Yes, we provide pre-arranged wheelchair assistance at major temples like Kashi Vishwanath (Varanasi) and Ram Mandir (Ayodhya), along with senior-friendly hotels and guides.
