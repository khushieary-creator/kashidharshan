What is Sugam VIP Darshan at Kashi Vishwanath Temple?

The Sugam VIP Darshan Pass is a special priority entry ticket issued by the Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple Trust. It allows pilgrims to skip the massive general queue and enter the temple sanctum (Garbhagriha) directly through a designated VIP gate with dedicated pandits assisting the darshan.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1. Can I get a VIP Darshan pass on the same day in Varanasi?

Same-day counter slots are limited. It is recommended to book at least 2 to 3 days in advance or book via Kashi Dharshan Yatra Packages.

Q2. Is phone allowed inside Kashi Vishwanath Corridor?

Mobile phones, cameras, leather items, and bags are prohibited inside the Garbhagriha. Free locker counters are provided at Gate No. 4.
