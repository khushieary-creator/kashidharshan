Varanasi (Kashi), the spiritual heart of India, is best experienced from the sacred waters of the River Ganges. While traditional hand-rowed wooden boats offer a classic charm, the introduction of **luxury double-decker cruise liners** has transformed how travelers explore the ancient city. A cruise ride allows you to cover all 84 ghats in centralized air-conditioned comfort, avoiding crowded walking steps. This guide covers booking portals, ticket prices, boarding ghats, and Ganga Aarti slots.

The Ganga Cruise Experience

Watching the sunrise during **Subah-e-Banaras** or seeing the evening ghats illuminated by oil lamps during the **Ganga Aarti** are bucket-list items. Luxury cruises feature double decks—an air-conditioned lower deck with large glass windows and an open upper deck for 360-degree photography. On-board guides narrate stories of Varanasi's historical ghats (like Assi Ghat, Manikarnika, and Dashashwamedh Ghat) as the vessel sails smoothly against the Ganga current.

Alaknanda Luxury Cruise Tickets

The **Alaknanda Cruise** is the pioneer of luxury sailing in Kashi. Operating since 2018, it is equipped with high-end safety gear, bio-toilets, and audio-visual setups. Key features include:

  • Morning Devotion Slot: Starts around 06:00 AM to 07:30 AM, taking you past the bathing ghats as the morning sun rises.
  • Evening Aarti Slot: Starts around 05:30 PM to 07:30 PM, anchoring directly in front of Dashashwamedh Ghat for the Ganga Aarti.
  • Route: Ravidas Ghat to Namo Ghat and back (covering the entire stretch of Varanasi's core ghats).

Bhagirathi & State Ro-Ro Vessels

To support green tourism, the state administration operates clean-fuel Ro-Ro passenger ferries (like the **Samrat** and **Bhagirathi** vessels). These are larger double-decker ships that carry hundreds of passengers simultaneously. They are highly budget-friendly and run multiple shuttle rounds daily between Namo Ghat and Ravidas Ghat, stopping at major intermediate boarding points.

Booking Tip: Online tickets open 30 days in advance. During peak tourist periods like Dev Deepawali, prices can surge significantly, and advance bookings are mandatory.
