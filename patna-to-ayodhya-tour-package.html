Patna to Ayodhya 2-Day Yatra Itinerary

A quick weekend itinerary designed for road/train travel from Patna.

Day 1 — Patna Departure & Ayodhya Arrival

Transit & Saryu Aarti

Depart early from Patna via direct train or private road cab. Check in to your hotel by afternoon. Spend the evening visiting Saryu River Ghat for the grand evening Aarti and enjoying Ram Ki Paidi.

Day 2 — VIP Temple Darshan & Return

Hanuman Garhi, Ram Mandir & Return drop

Visit Hanuman Garhi temple in the morning. Enter the Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir using our VIP Sugam passes to bypass queues. Visit Kanak Bhawan. Post lunch, check out and return to Patna by train or cab.
