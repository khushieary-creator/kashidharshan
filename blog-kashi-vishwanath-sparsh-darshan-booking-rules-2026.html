Kashi Vishwanath Sparsh Darshan Booking 2026: Timings, Ticket Price & Abhishek Rules

Touching the sacred Svayambhu Jyotirlinga of Lord Shiva and offering sacred Ganga Jal and Bilva Patra with your own hands is the ultimate spiritual blessing of Kashi Yatra. However, strict crowd-control rules and dress codes apply inside the Garbhagriha. Here is your definitive guide to Kashi Vishwanath Sparsh Darshan timings, ticket procedures, and Abhishek guidelines for 2026.

1. What is Sparsh Darshan vs. Jhanki Darshan?

There are two primary modes of taking darshan at Shri Kashi Vishwanath Dham:

  • Jhanki Darshan (Glimpse from Distance): Devotees view Lord Shiva from outside the silver doorway (Chaukhat) and pour sacred water into an external brass conduit (Argha) that leads to the Jyotirlinga. No special dress code is enforced.
  • Sparsh Darshan (Physical Touch & Abhishek): Devotees enter the Garbhagriha sanctum sanctorum, sit beside the Golden Jyotirlinga, touch the holy Lingam with their forehead or hands, and offer Panchamrit Abhishek under the guidance of Temple Archakas.

2. Official Sparsh Darshan Daily Time Slots

Sparsh Darshan is permitted during specific windows when regular Aarti ceremonies are not taking place:

Night Sparsh 08:30 PM – 09:00 PM Brief 30-minute window between Saptarishi Aarti and Night Shringara Aarti.

3. Mandatory Dress Code for Garbhagriha Entry

Security and temple trustees enforce strict adherence to Vedic attire for Sparsh Darshan:

  • Men: Must wear clean, traditional unstitched Dhoti (white, yellow, or saffron) with Kurta or Angavastram. Trousers, jeans, Bermuda shorts, t-shirts, and leather belts are strictly banned inside the sanctum.
  • Women: Must wear Indian traditional Saree or full Salwar Kameez with Dupatta. Western attire like jeans or skirts is not permitted.
  • Footwear & Belongings: Shoes, socks, smartwatches, smartphones, leather wallets, and purses must be left in digital lockers at Gate No. 2 or Gate No. 4 before entering the queue.

4. How to Book Rudrabhishekam with Sparsh Darshan

For pilgrims seeking a dedicated personal Vedic ceremony, booking a formal Rudrabhishekam is the most auspicious option:

  • 1 Shastri Abhishek (₹500): Performed by 1 temple priest; includes Jalabhishekam and chanting of Sri Rudram.
  • 5 Shastri Abhishek (₹2,100): Performed by 5 Vedic priests with detailed Panchamrit Snan, Bilva Patra archana, and Mahaprasad.
  • Laghurudra / Ekadasha Rudra (₹7,500 – ₹15,000): Elaborate Yajna performed by 11 archakas for planetary dosha removal and spiritual liberation.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: Is Sparsh Darshan allowed on Mondays and during Sawan?

Due to heavy crowds of over 200,000 devotees, Sparsh Darshan is strictly suspended on Mondays during the month of Sawan and on Maha Shivratri. Devotees are only offered Jhanki Darshan via Argha on those days.

Q: Can senior citizens get wheelchair support for Sparsh Darshan?

Yes, battery-operated golf carts and wheelchairs with attendants operate from Gate 4 (Ganga Riverfront) and Gate 2 (Saraswati Gate) right up to the Garbhagriha entrance. Read our complete Kashi Vishwanath Tour Package for senior citizen assistance details.

Q: What materials can I take inside for Abhishek?

Only pure Ganga water in a copper or brass vessel (Lota), fresh Bilva leaves, milk, and flowers purchased from authorized temple stalls are permitted inside the Garbhagriha.
