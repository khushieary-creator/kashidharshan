4 Days · 3 Nights

Ayodhya Varanasi Tour Package

Connect the two great pillars of Northern India's spiritual heritage in a single seamless itinerary. Experience Lord Ram's birthplace and Lord Shiva's eternal city with fully guided transfers and VIP darshans.

4-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Welcome to Ayodhya

Ayodhya Arrival & Saryu Aarti

Arrival at Ayodhya airport or station, transfer to your hotel. Spend the evening visiting Ram ki Paidi for the sunset Saryu River Aarti.

Day 2 — Ayodhya Darshan & Varanasi Drive

Ram Mandir & Transfer to Kashi

Perform early morning darshan at Hanuman Garhi and the grand Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir. Visit Kanak Bhawan, then check out and drive to Varanasi (approx. 5.5 hours in your private AC car). Check in at your Varanasi hotel.

Day 3 — Varanasi Darshan & Ganga Aarti

Kashi Vishwanath & Ganga River

Visit the Kashi Vishwanath Temple corridor, Annapurna Mandir, and Kal Bhairav Temple. In the evening, witness the world-famous Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat from a private boat.

Day 4 — Sunrise Boat Ride & Departure

Subah-e-Banaras & Sarnath

Experience a peaceful sunrise boat ride along the historic ghats, visit Sarnath (where Buddha preached his first sermon), and head to the airport/station for departure.

FAQ — Kashi Ayodhya Yatra

Which city should we arrive in first?+
You can arrive in either Ayodhya or Varanasi. However, arriving in Ayodhya, exploring the temples, and then driving down to Varanasi for the final departure is the most popular and geographically seamless route.
Are Kashi Vishwanath VIP darshan passes included?+
Yes, standard entry assistance is included. Standard and Premium tiers include coordination for sugam darshan tickets in Varanasi.
