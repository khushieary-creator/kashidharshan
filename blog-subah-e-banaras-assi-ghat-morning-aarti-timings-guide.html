Subah-e-Banaras Assi Ghat Timings 2026: Morning Ganga Aarti, Sunrise Boat Tour & Schedule

Complete pilgrim guide to experiencing the ethereal dawn of Varanasi at Assi Ghat. Morning Ganga Aarti timings, Vedic Hawan rituals, sunrise boat ride fares, classical Raga recitals, and famous Banarasi morning breakfast spots.

Why You Must Wake Up at 5:00 AM for Subah-e-Banaras

Legend has it that Mark Twain once wrote: "Benares is older than history, older than tradition, older even than legend, and looks twice as old as all of them put together." Nowhere is this timeless eternity felt more deeply than at Assi Ghat at dawn.

Known as "Subah-e-Banaras", this daily cultural and spiritual tradition weaves together Vedic yagya, rhythmic chanting, multi-tiered brass oil lamps, morning ragas, and a sunrise boat cruise across the misty waters of Mother Ganga.

1. Subah-e-Banaras Daily Morning Schedule (2026 Updated)

The entire program is conducted daily on the raised concrete podium of Assi Ghat and follows a strict time-honored sequence:

Time Slot Ritual / Event Key Spiritual Highlights
05:00 AM – 05:30 AM Vedic Yagya & Hawan Young Batuk Brahmins chant Rigvedic and Shukla Yajurvedic hymns around sacred fire altars.
05:30 AM – 06:15 AM Grand Sunrise Ganga Aarti 5 priests perform synchronized brass lamp aarti facing the rising sun as conch shells resound.
06:15 AM – 07:00 AM Classical Music Recital (Raga) Vocal classical music, Shehnai, Flute, and Sitar performances by Banaras Gharana maestros.
07:00 AM – 07:45 AM Open Yoga & Pranayama Guided public yoga and meditation session by expert instructors. Free for all attendees.
06:00 AM – 08:00 AM Sunrise Ganga Boat Ride Cruise from Assi Ghat to Dashashwamedh, Manikarnika, and Scindia Ghats to view morning snan rituals.
Note: In peak winter (Dec–Jan), morning timings shift slightly by 30 minutes (05:30 AM start) to align with dawn sunrise.

2. Assi Ghat Sunrise Boat Tour: Options & Fare Rates (2026)

Right after watching the Aarti, boarding a boat at Assi Ghat to cruise past the 84 ghats is the highlight of every Varanasi trip. You get to photograph the iconic sunrise over the eastern banks, watch sadhus performing Surya Namaskar, and see life unfold along the riverfront.

Traditional Experience

Private Wooden Row Boat

₹1,000 – ₹1,500 / boat (1–4 Persons)

Quiet, motor-less wooden boat steered by an experienced local boatman. Perfect for silent photography, bird watching, and spiritual reflection.

  • Duration: 1.5 to 2 Hours
  • Route: Assi Ghat ➔ Dashashwamedh ➔ Manikarnika ➔ Return
Family & Group Choice

Private Motorboat (Speedboat)

₹2,500 – ₹3,500 / boat (Up to 10 Persons)

Faster, spacious motorboat covering the entire 7 km ghat stretch from Assi Ghat all the way to Namo Ghat (Khidkiya Ghat) and back comfortably.

  • Duration: 2 Hours
  • Covers: All 84 Ghats including Namo Ghat

3. Post-Aarti Breakfast: Iconic Banarasi Morning Delicacies

No morning in Varanasi is complete without treating your tastebuds to authentic street breakfast right behind Assi Ghat:

  • Kachori Sabzi & Jalebi: Fresh, piping-hot round kachoris served with spicy aloo-chana gravy and crispy desi ghee jalebis at Pahalwan Lassi or Ram Bhandar.
  • Winter Malaiyo (Makhan Malai): Available exclusively from October through February, this cloud-like saffron milk foam flavored with pistachios and almonds melts on your tongue.
  • Kullhad Masala Chai & Toast: Fresh ginger-cardamom tea brewed in clay kullhads at the legendary Pappu Tea Stall near Assi.
  • Blue Lassi / Assi Pomegranate Lassi: Thick churned curd topped with fresh malai and fruit crushes.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (Pilgrim FAQs)

How far is Assi Ghat from Kashi Vishwanath Temple / Godowlia?
Assi Ghat is approximately 3.2 km south of Godowlia and Kashi Vishwanath Gate 4. An e-rickshaw takes about 10–12 minutes (₹20 per seat or ₹150 for private hire). Alternatively, you can take a morning boat from Dashashwamedh Ghat to Assi Ghat.
Is advance booking necessary for the sunrise boat ride?
During peak tourist months (October through March) and festivals (like Dev Deepawali and Mahashivratri), pre-booking your boat guarantees fixed government-approved rates and punctual 05:45 AM departure.
Can senior citizens participate in morning yoga?
Yes! The yoga sessions include gentle pranayama, breathing exercises, and mild stretches suitable for senior citizens. Mats are laid out on the stone steps, and participation is completely voluntary.
