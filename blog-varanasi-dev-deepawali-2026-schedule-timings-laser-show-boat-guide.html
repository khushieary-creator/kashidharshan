Varanasi Dev Deepawali 2026: 84 Ghats Diya Timings, Laser Show & Boat Rates

Complete, verified master schedule for the Festival of Gods on Tuesday, 24 November 2026 (Kartik Purnima). Official 84 ghats diya lighting timings, Chet Singh Ghat 3D laser projection show schedule, green fireworks, official boat fare caps, and traffic survival rules.

🪔

Direct Answer: What Time Does Dev Deepawali Lighting Start in Varanasi?

On Tuesday, 24 November 2026, the ceremonial lighting of the first earthen lamp (Akash Deep) begins at 05:15 PM at Dashashwamedh and Panchganga Ghats. Between 05:30 PM and 06:15 PM, all 84 ghats across 7 kilometers are fully lit with over 12 Lakh diyas. The Grand Maha Aarti starts at 06:00 PM, followed by the Chet Singh Ghat 3D Laser Show at 07:00 PM and green fireworks across the sand dunes at 07:30 PM.

⚠️ Crucial Boat Boarding Rule: All tourists with boat reservations must board by 03:30 PM to 04:00 PM. Water police seals river boarding points at 04:30 PM sharp to maintain safety barricades, and latecomers will NOT be permitted to board!

4. Varanasi Traffic & Pedestrian Survival Plan (24 Nov)

🚫 Complete No-Vehicle Zone

From 02:00 PM to midnight, all cars, auto-rickshaws, and two-wheelers are prohibited beyond Godowlia, Maidagin, Sonarpura, and Assi. Be prepared to walk 15 to 20 minutes to reach ghat stone steps.

🅿️ Designated Parking Hubs

Park your private vehicle or taxi at Town Hall Maidagin, Beniya Bagh Underground Parking, or Sampurnanand Sports Stadium Sigra, then take e-rickshaws up to the traffic diversion barrier.

🧥 Riverfront Warm Clothing

November nights on the open river are significantly chilly. Temperatures on water drop to 13°C by 08:00 PM. Carry woolen shawls, windcheaters, and ear caps especially for elderly parents and kids.

Frequently Asked Questions (Dev Deepawali 2026 FAQs)

What is the exact date and diya lighting timing for Dev Deepawali 2026 in Varanasi?

Dev Deepawali will be celebrated on Tuesday, 24 November 2026 (Kartik Purnima). The ceremonial lighting of the first earthen lamp takes place at 05:15 PM at Dashashwamedh Ghat, and by 05:30 PM to 06:15 PM, all 84 ghats are completely illuminated with over 12 Lakh glowing diyas.

What time does the 3D Laser Show start at Chet Singh Ghat during Dev Deepawali?

The Chet Singh Ghat 3D Projection Mapping & Laser Light Sound Show will have two shows on 24 November 2026: the first show at 07:00 PM and the second show at 08:30 PM. The 20-minute show narrates the story of the descent of River Ganga and Lord Shiva defeating demon Tripurasur.

What time should visitors board a boat on Dev Deepawali?

Visitors must board their pre-reserved boat by 03:30 PM to 04:00 PM. By 04:30 PM, the water police stops boats from leaving boarding points, and latecomers risk missing riverfront viewing completely.

How much does a boat ride cost on Dev Deepawali 2026 in Varanasi?

During Dev Deepawali, private traditional row boats cost ₹4,000 to ₹7,000. Private motorboats (10–15 persons) range between ₹14,000 and ₹22,000, and shared upper-deck seats on double-decker VIP Bajras cost ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 per person.

What is the Ganga Mahotsav 2026 schedule in Varanasi?

Ganga Mahotsav 2026 takes place from Saturday, 21 November to Tuesday, 24 November 2026 at Rajghat and Assi Ghat. It features classical vocal, sitar, shehnai, and kathak recitals by national maestros each evening from 05:30 PM to 10:00 PM.

Are vehicles allowed near Godowlia and the Ghats on Dev Deepawali?

No. From 02:00 PM on 24 November 2026 until midnight, all roads leading to the ghats (including Godowlia, Maidagin, Assi, Sonarpura, and Sigra) are strict pedestrian-only zones. Vehicles must park at designated lots such as Town Hall, Beniya Bagh, or Sampurnanand Stadium.
